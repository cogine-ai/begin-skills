# Typography case notices

This optional collection preserves a reviewed typography refinement of the existing fictional Side A listening evening. It contains the complete home, browser-local itinerary and guide source, plus six fresh native desktop and phone openings. It is a refinement of an existing composition; these resources were not generated as a new scenario from an updated Skill. See the [worked method](../../references/typography.md) and [site credits](side-a/site/NOTICES.md).

## Original website and scope

The Side A identity, programme, guide, itinerary behavior, HTML, CSS and icon are original Begin study material. The revised source is copied byte for byte from its final freeze. The earlier transfer-cases version remains separate and unchanged. The source's historical references to surrounding trial records describe its development; raw trial records are excluded from this portable collection. `PROVENANCE.json` retains their exact identities and repository-relative locations.

The itinerary uses browser-local storage for a fictional evening. It makes no booking, ticket sale, payment, server submission or external calendar change. Rendering observations and the independently reviewed type choices have local scopes. They do not prove general first-draft quality, host compatibility, visitor preference or a commercial outcome.

## Bundled display font

Fraunces, by Phaedra Charles and Flavia Zimbardi / Undercase Type, is the complete variable Roman TTF. Its bytes are unchanged from the [Google Fonts distribution](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fraunces). The [upstream project](https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces), exact fetched source URLs and file identities remain in `side-a/site/FONT_SOURCE.json`. The font retains its complete [SIL Open Font License 1.1 and copyright notice](side-a/site/fonts/fraunces-OFL.txt). Weight, optical size, softness and wonky forms are rendering choices; no font conversion, subsetting or modification occurred during curation.

## Bundled body and utility font

The internal family **Fieldnotes Sans** is Begin's existing derived Source Sans 3 resource. Its historical `fonts/source-sans-3.woff2` filename is retained; the revised CSS accurately names the internal family. The font and complete [Adobe copyright and OFL notice](side-a/site/fonts/source-sans-3-OFL.txt) are byte-identical to the earlier Side A resource. The prior verified internal names and exact retained identities are recorded in `PROVENANCE.json`.
