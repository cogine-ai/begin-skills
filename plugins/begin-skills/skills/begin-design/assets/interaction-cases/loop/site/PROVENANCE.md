# LOOP source provenance

## Brief and original work

LOOP began with an original functional brief for a fictional neighbourhood refill shop. That brief supplied the six complete product names, priced measures, prices and container notes. It required a local collection list, bounded quantities, integer-pence calculations, browser recovery, a readable download and a practical container guide.

The three pages, composition, six packaging illustrations, copy, styles and interaction implementation were authored for this example. Catalogue facts remain inspectable in `catalogue-source.json`. The website has no transmitted order, live stock, collection booking or payment flow.

## Type and design process

Begin’s design, frontend and quality methods informed an original comparison of two type systems on the actual long product names, container instructions, prices and units. Native desktop and phone comparisons at 1440, 390 and 320 pixels informed the selection of Bricolage Grotesque with Work Sans.

The first complete source was frozen before its first full-site preview. Later independent review and actual local interaction checks led to a refined phone review path, protected working drafts, clearer price/quantity grouping and a practical guide opening with a stable weighing relationship. The refined pages, populated request and actual downloaded file were checked at all three widths. Raw study records remain outside this portable example.

## Font material

Bricolage Grotesque and Work Sans were copied unchanged from Begin’s available local font resources. Exact binary SHA-256 hashes and byte counts are in `FONT-COPIES.json`. Full applicable font licenses, original source notices and pinned upstream font metadata are retained in `assets/fonts/`. These records preserve the fonts’ identity and rights independently of this example’s original writing and implementation.
