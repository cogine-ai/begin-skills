The container guide

Bring a good container

Weigh it empty, keep liquids sealed, and separate food from household refills.

01

Weigh it empty

Start with a clean, empty container. Weigh it before adding anything and note its empty weight — often called the tare.

For goods sold by weight, the container weight is deducted so only the contents are counted. Keep that weight visible or easy to find when you fill.

02

Give liquids a tight lid

Check the lid and seal before you leave home. Carry bottles upright and use a bag that will hold them steady.

Match the amount in your list to the space in your container. For 1,250 ml of olive oil, bring that much empty capacity, in one bottle or several. A pump bottle or a closed jar can hold hand soap.

03

Keep food and household refills apart

Use food containers for olive oil, oats, coffee and apricots. Dry foods need dry containers so their contents stay dry.

Laundry powder belongs in a separate, dry non-food container with a clear label. Keep it apart from food. Check an old container’s suitability with the shop before filling it.

A couple of useful checks

Can I split one quantity between containers?

Yes, your preparation list can describe the combined quantity. Bring enough total empty capacity and keep each container suitable for its contents. The list is a local example; it does not reserve stock or arrange a collection.

Does downloading my list send it to the shop?

No. The download gives you a readable collection request to keep. No order is transmitted, no collection is booked and no payment is taken. You can reopen the file to check your quantities and container notes.

How do I come back to my list?

The collection page keeps your working draft in this browser automatically when storage is available. “Save list” keeps a separate recovery copy, and “Recover saved list” restores that copy. If storage is unavailable, keep the current page open and download your request before leaving.
