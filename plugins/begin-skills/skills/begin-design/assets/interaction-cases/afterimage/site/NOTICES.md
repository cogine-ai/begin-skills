# AFTERIMAGE notices

AFTERIMAGE, the festival, artists, installations and Bellweather Dock are fictional demonstration material supplied in the task brief. The site does not offer a live event, ticket sales or bookings.

The HTML, CSS, JavaScript and SVG illustrations were originally authored for this independent local study. The SVG files interpret the supplied names, light, sound, water reflection and industrial space; they are not documentation of actual installations, photographs or a geographic map. No third-party artwork, code or implementation was imported.

## Fonts

Newsreader and Work Sans are supplied as unmodified font binaries under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Full copyright and license notices are included at `fonts/OFL-Newsreader.txt` and `fonts/OFL-WorkSans.txt`. Local source paths and SHA-256 integrity records are in the study’s `type-probe/FONT_PROVENANCE.json` and `PROVENANCE.md`. These fonts retain their own licenses.

## Local functionality

The planner uses browser local storage and prepares a readable text file on the visitor’s device. No visitor content is sent to a server, and there is no account, payment, ticket, live stock, booking or analytics integration.
