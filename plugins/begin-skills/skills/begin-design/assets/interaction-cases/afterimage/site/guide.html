Choose an evening

19–21 March 2027Friday to Sunday
18:00–23:00Every evening
Bellweather DockSix installations

All six installations can be visited during the evening. Pick the day that suits you in the programme, then add the works you want to see.

The displayed durations estimate time with each installation. They are not booked appointment times. Your total does not include walking, breaks or time spent exploring the dock.

Three works are outdoors: Low Tide Choir, Signal Garden and After the Rain. The other three are indoors. Read the individual access notes before choosing your evening.

03

Keep your plan close

Your chosen day and works are saved in this browser after each change. Return to the programme in the same browser to pick up where you left off. “Save on this device” lets you retry if saving was unavailable.

Use “Download my plan” to prepare a readable text copy with every selected work, artist, place, estimate and access note. The file also includes the day, opening hours and your total estimated installation time.

If browser storage is unavailable, you can keep working in the current tab. Download before leaving, or open “Read or copy the complete plan” and select its text. A saved plan cannot move between browsers or devices automatically.

“Clear plan” removes the saved choices on this device when browser storage is available. A downloaded copy stays wherever you saved it.
