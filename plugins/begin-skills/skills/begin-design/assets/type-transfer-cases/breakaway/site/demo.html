The calculation

Distance ÷ target pace × 60 gives the moving duration in minutes, rounded to the nearest whole minute. Add that duration to the start time for the estimated finish.

If the calculation crosses midnight, the finish is marked next day. Try 60 km at 24 km/h starting at 23:00: 150 minutes, finishing at 01:30 next day.
