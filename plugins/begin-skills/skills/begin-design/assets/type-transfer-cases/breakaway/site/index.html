01 / The useful bit

Your pace,
on paper

Before the ride comes a small calculation. Give the worksheet a distance, a target pace and a start time. It turns those three numbers into a moving duration and a finish time.

A pause for coffee is yours to add. The sheet assumes a constant pace and counts riding time only.

03 / A note from the club↗

The long way is a good way

Breakaway is an imagined club built around one real pleasure: thinking about the next ride. Our route book is drawn, our meeting place is invented, and our pace worksheet is useful on its own.

Make a sheet, change the numbers, keep it in your browser, or take a text copy. The small ritual is the point.
