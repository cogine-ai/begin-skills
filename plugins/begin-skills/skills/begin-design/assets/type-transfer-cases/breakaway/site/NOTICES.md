# Breakaway credits and notices

Breakaway is an original fictional cycling-club website made for a local Begin dev.11 trial on 11 October 2026. Club names, route names, writing, SVG route artwork and marks are original example material. No real club, venue, offered route or scheduled ride is represented. The diagram is invented and is not navigable.

The local worksheet calculates riding duration at a constant target pace and an estimated finish time. Stops and delays are excluded. It makes no route-safety or fitness assessment. No booking, message, account, payment or telemetry exists.

## Fonts

Barlow and Barlow Condensed were designed by Jeremy Tribby. Copyright 2017 The Barlow Project Authors (https://github.com/jpt/barlow).

Unmodified files downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository:

- `assets/fonts/BarlowCondensed-ExtraBold.ttf`: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold, display at actual weight 800.
- `assets/fonts/Barlow-Regular.ttf`: Barlow Regular, body and utility at actual weight 400.
- `assets/fonts/Barlow-SemiBold.ttf`: Barlow SemiBold, labels and controls at actual weight 600.

These font files use the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The complete copyright notices and license terms are retained in `assets/fonts/BarlowCondensed-OFL.txt` and `assets/fonts/Barlow-OFL.txt`. Exact original download URLs, byte lengths and SHA-256 identities are recorded in `FONT_SOURCE.json`. No font file was converted, subset, modified or renamed internally.

## Method references

The site uses methods from the frozen Begin dev.11 portable input. Bundled example captures informed composition and guide decisions. No photograph or graphic from those examples was reused. The original SVG route and mark require no third-party media license.
