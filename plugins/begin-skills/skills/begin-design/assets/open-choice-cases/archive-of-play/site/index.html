AP01

Demo date 1932

Turnfold

A fold, a move, a way home.

Players
2–4
Play time
10 min
Read the complete rules +

Fold the board into four fields. On each turn, move one counter to a neighbouring field. The first player to return all three counters to the starting field wins.

AP02

Demo date 1967

Clockwork Meadow

A landscape that turns with you.

Players
2–6
Play time
15 min
Read the complete rules +

Lay the six meadow tiles in a ring. Rotate one tile on each turn and follow its marked path with a wooden piece. Collect three different leaves before returning to the gate.

AP03

Demo date 1984

The Quiet Line

Twelve stones. Four small endings.

Players
1–3
Play time
20 min
Read the complete rules +

Place twelve stones along the printed line. Move one stone past exactly one neighbour each turn. Finish with four separate groups of three stones.

AP04

Demo date 1951

Rattle, Roll & Remember

Listen, look, then look away.

Players
3–6
Play time
12 min
Read the complete rules +

Roll the three marked blocks and name their symbols. Cover them, then repeat the symbols in reverse order. Add one token after each complete answer.

AP05

Demo date 1978

Les petits passages

Find a route through four rooms.

Players
2–5
Play time
18 min
Read the complete rules +

Connect the small passage cards without covering an entrance. Move a paper figure through one open passage on each turn. The first figure to visit all four rooms wins.

Use in a handout ↗
AP06

Demo date 1996

A Map of Almost Everything

Give a place a name. Join it to another.

Players
2–4
Play time
25 min
Read the complete rules +

Draw one shared map with the supplied shape cards. Each player adds a place and gives it a name. Finish by finding a continuous route through every named place.
