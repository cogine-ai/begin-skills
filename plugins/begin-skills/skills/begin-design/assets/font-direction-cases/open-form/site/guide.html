Try a four-session route

If you have open choices you want to keep, prepare and keep them first. Then use Reset open choices. Reset does not remove an existing kept copy.

  1. Open My route. Choose Layer lab, Shape shift, Color exchange and Open edition. Leave Roll & repeat unchecked.
  2. Enter these values:
Route name
Ink & shape
My note
Bring a flat folder for the prints.

Choose Prepare my route.

Expected result

Ink & shape, marked Current. Four sessions, 345 workshop min, 105 gap min, 09:30–17:00. Your note appears after the final session.

Workshop minutes count the selected sessions. Gap minutes count the time between them; they include a lunch break.

Resolve an overlap

Keep the four-session choices. Also choose Roll & repeat.

The planner reports two overlaps: Layer lab with Roll & repeat by 15 min, and Roll & repeat with Shape shift by 30 min.

Choose Prepare my route. It refuses the conflicting choices. Your earlier route stays visible and is marked Earlier choices; Keep and Download are unavailable.

Uncheck Roll & repeat. The earlier text now matches the open choices again. Choose Prepare if you also changed the name or note.

After any edit

Changing a session, name or note preserves the previous text. Prepare again to create a current copy before keeping or downloading.

Keep, recover & export

  1. With a Current route, choose Keep in this browser. This replaces any earlier kept route on this site.
  2. Choose Download text to get open-form-route.txt. The text uses the same current choices shown in the prepared route.
  3. Choose Reset open choices. The open selection clears and the prepared view disappears. Your kept route remains.
  4. Choose Recover kept route. It replaces the open name, note and choices with the kept copy, then restores its Current text.

Reloading the page opens empty choices; a kept route remains available through Recover. Unsaved choices and prepared text are lost on reload.

Forget kept route deletes the local kept copy. It leaves your open choices and prepared text unchanged. A downloaded file is separate and must be removed from your files if you no longer want it.

Local data & limits

Where does the route go?
Only into this browser's local storage when you choose Keep, or into a text file when you choose Download. Nothing is sent to a server or an organizer.
Can I book a place?
No. Open Form is a fictional local demonstration. Preparation and keeping do not reserve seats.
What counts as a clash?
Selected sessions overlap by one or more minutes. One session may end at the exact time another begins.
What if keeping fails?
The planner keeps the open text and reports the problem. Download a text copy. Private browsing, browser cleanup or another origin can make a kept route unavailable.
Which times and limits apply?
Saturday 24 October 2026, venue-local times at Common Press, North Hall. Route names require 1–60 characters after trimming; notes allow up to 500. At least one session is required.
