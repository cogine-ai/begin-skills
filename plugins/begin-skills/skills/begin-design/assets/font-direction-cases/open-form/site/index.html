The programme

Choose the methods that interest you. Materials are provided; each session has its own access notes.

01
09:30–10:45

Linocut

Layer lab

Cut a small shape. Let the same block make three different impressions.

Soft-cut block, water-based ink and A4 paper.

Seated benches. Cutting tools are used with an instructor.

02
10:30–11:45

Monoprint

Roll & repeat

Work directly on the plate. Pull a sequence of unpredictable textures.

Gel plates, rollers and recycled paper.

Step-free studio. Latex-free gloves are available.

03
11:15–12:45

Stencil

Shape shift

Fold, rotate and layer a stencil to build a set of bold forms.

Paper stencils, sponges and two ink colors.

Seated and standing workstations. No blades are required.

04
13:30–15:00

Two-color risograph

Color exchange

Swap one color with someone else. See how the whole print changes.

Two prepared masters and A3 sheets.

A quieter table is available. The printer makes brief mechanical noise.

05
15:30–17:00

Letterpress

Open edition

Set a word in wood type. Print a small edition to take home.

Wood type, hand press and heavy paper.

Adjustable table and seated operation. Staff help with press pressure.

Leave with ink on your hands

A small block becomes a pattern. A shifted stencil becomes a new shape. An ordinary word becomes something you can hold.

Open Form brings five printmaking methods into one shared studio. Choose a route with room to experiment, talk and let the prints dry.
