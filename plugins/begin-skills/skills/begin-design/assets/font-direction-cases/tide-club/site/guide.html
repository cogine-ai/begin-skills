02Prepare and check

  1. Set Plan name to Easy water.
  2. Set One thing to focus on to Keep the exhale unhurried.
  3. Press Prepare my plan.

The result should be Current, with 3 sessions, 135 pool minutes and 35 minutes between. The breaks are 15 minutes and 20 minutes. The sheet should read:

The sessions and venue are illustrative. Preparing this sheet does not book a lane or reserve a session.

03Keep or download

Press Keep in this browser to save this current plan locally. Your saved plan should say One plan kept. Keeping a second plan replaces the earlier saved copy.

Press Download text to request tide-club-plan.txt. Its content matches the current sheet, with a final line break. Downloading leaves your open and saved plan unchanged.

If local storage is unavailable, keep the downloaded file. The planner explains a storage failure and continues to prepare and download.

04Recover your plan

  1. After keeping Easy water, reload the planner page.
  2. The open choices start empty. The saved copy still says One plan kept.
  3. Press Recover kept plan.

The three selected sessions, plan name and focus note return. The sheet becomes Current with the same 135 pool minutes and 35 minutes between.

Recovery replaces the open choices with the saved copy. There is one saved plan per browser origin; use the same browser and site address.

05Change or reset

Untick A calmer catch, a longer stroke. The earlier sheet remains visible and says Choices changed. Keep and Download pause until you press Prepare my plan again. The new two-session plan has 85 pool minutes and 15 minutes between.

A calmer catch, a longer stroke and Flip turns without the rush overlap. If both are selected, preparation explains the clash and preserves the earlier sheet. Untick one to continue.

Reset choices clears the open selections, name, note and sheet. It keeps the saved copy. Forget saved plan removes that copy and leaves the open choices and sheet unchanged.

06Your plan stays here

The example sends no plan data to a server. A plan is saved only when you press Keep in this browser. The browser can remove local storage when you clear site data; private browsing and storage restrictions can also affect it.

Use fictional notes while exploring. A downloaded file can be moved or shared outside the browser, so check its contents before sharing it.
