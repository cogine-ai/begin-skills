# Tide Club notices

Tide Club is an original fictional community swimming-clinic study. The names, dates, venue, session descriptions, pool drawing, logo, page design, planner source and guide were created for this internal Begin study. No actual swimming service, booking, health guidance or performance claim is offered.

## Fonts

Unmodified Google Fonts binaries are stored locally. Full licenses and source metadata are adjacent to them.

- **Anton**, Regular 400, by Vernon Adams. Binary: `fonts/Anton-Regular.ttf`. Complete license: [OFL-Anton.txt](fonts/OFL-Anton.txt). Primary source: [Google Fonts Anton repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/anton).
- **Work Sans**, upright variable weight 100–900, by Wei Huang. This study uses 400, 500, 600 and 700. Binary: `fonts/WorkSans-Variable.ttf`. Complete license: [OFL-WorkSans.txt](fonts/OFL-WorkSans.txt). Primary source: [Google Fonts Work Sans repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/worksans).

No font binary, lettering, graphic, photograph or page source was copied from Nike or AKU. Their primary pages informed limited typographic and content decisions. The study records those decisions separately.
