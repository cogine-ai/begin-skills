# Original work and third-party type

Begin-authored code, prose, drawings and recorded browser captures in these three examples use the pack's MIT terms. MORA's product identity and catalogue are fictional. PHASE uses an original vector model. DOWN TO THE MOON is an independent educational reader with original schematics and concise paraphrases of its linked NASA primary sources; no NASA images, logos, audio or website code are included. Its diagram is conceptual, not a flight simulation.

Fonts remain under their full SIL Open Font License notices. They are unchanged local copies from existing Begin resources. The pack's MIT grant does not replace those licenses. Each complete site carries its own notices and source identities.

- [MORA notices](mora/site/NOTICES.md)
- [Reader notices](apollo/site/NOTICES.md)
- [PHASE notices](phase/site/NOTICES.md)

The accompanying motion files are actual browser recordings of these original local sites. They are examples of the inspected interaction, not physical-device performance benchmarks. Raw first sources, probes, full reviews and other recordings remain outside this delivered collection.

In the complete sites' font/provenance records, `file` paths use the site root. A `frozen_source` value names a historical research input or stage outside the copied site; it is not a runtime dependency. The current collection manifest binds every delivered local file to its exact bytes. Site notices written during generation can retain a pre-review evidence boundary; `CHECKS.json` records the later selected phase's observations.
