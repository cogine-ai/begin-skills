## Mission facts

Facts are concise paraphrases of the task's primary-source material, verified by the trial coordinator on 15 October 2026:

1. https://www.nasa.gov/mission/apollo-11/

## Local execution

There is no analytics, upload, remote runtime dependency or backend. Browser storage holds an optional local draft and kept chapter/note. Text downloads are generated on the device. These runtime statements are source-level properties until the first source's browser review.
