A seat for the
unhurried city

On the corner of Bell Street, a bench faces away from the road. It offers no view worth photographing. A young plane tree, a brick wall, and the back door of a bakery are its whole horizon.

Most places in a city ask something of us. A shop expects a purchase; a platform expects a departure. A pause becomes public when the place allows us to stay without explaining why.

Your thinking · Example note

This is the difference between a place to pass through and a place to belong. Keep the permission to linger in the design.

Attached to “A pause becomes public…”

The bench makes that modest offer. You can sit with a bag of oranges, reread a message, or wait for the feeling that you are ready to walk again.
