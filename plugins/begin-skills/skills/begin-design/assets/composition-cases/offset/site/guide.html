What is saved

Shortlist
Adding or removing a study immediately saves its identity in this browser. The list keeps the order in which you added studies.
Draft fields
Your name, optional email and brief are saved only when you choose Save draft. Changing a shortlist never silently saves unfinished field edits.
Prepared result
Prepare enquiry draft uses the current fields and current shortlist. The result is not saved after reload. Editing a field or changing the shortlist removes the old result and asks you to prepare again.
Reset local data
Reset clears the saved shortlist, saved fields and the current task's prepared result. The selected study stays open. Reload other open Offset pages to read the empty state.
Browser setup
JavaScript operates the local controls. Open the three pages at the same site address to keep their saved data together.
Storage boundary
There is no account, server, shared database or enquiry recipient. Local data belongs to this browser and site address. Different ports or browsers have separate data.
