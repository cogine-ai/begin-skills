01 / Studio

A small practice in deliberate shifts

Offset is a fictional studio exploring how type, color and simple systems give an idea a recognisable shape. These six self-initiated studies move from loud posters to quieter print.

  • Type as structureA word can set a pace, create a route, or leave room to pause.
  • Marks with a methodA waveform, a fold, a sun — small ideas that can hold a system together.
  • Print as an objectScale, sequence and the relationship between a cover and its surround.
