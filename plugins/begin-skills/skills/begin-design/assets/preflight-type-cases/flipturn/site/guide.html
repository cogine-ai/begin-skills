Find the four pairs

  1. Open Play. Eight numbered tiles start face down. The practice name above them identifies your current circuit.
  2. Choose one tile. Its picture and full name appear. Choose a different tile to make one attempt.
  3. If the names match, both tiles stay face up and show Matched. Those tiles are finished and cannot be chosen again.
  4. If the names differ, both remain visible. Look at them for as long as you like. Choose Turn these tiles back to continue.
  5. Find all four pairs. Your complete record below the board includes every attempt, its tile numbers and names, the final move count, and completion.

The names let you recognize a pair without relying only on its picture or color. There is no timer. Choosing the same tile twice does not count a move.

Try a six-move first circuit

Use a fresh page or Restart this circuit on circuit 01. The first board is reproducible: Comet, Jellyfish, Lantern, Octopus, Lantern, Comet, Octopus, Jellyfish in tiles 1 through 8.

Choose the two tile numbers in each row. After each of the first two rows, choose Turn these tiles back. The next four attempts find all the pairs.

    Comet + JellyfishTiles 1 + 2 · No match · turn them back
  2. 02
    Lantern + OctopusTiles 3 + 4 · No match · turn them back
  3. 03
    Comet + CometTiles 1 + 6 · Pair found · 1 / 4
  4. 04
    Jellyfish + JellyfishTiles 2 + 8 · Pair found · 2 / 4
  5. 05

  6. 06
    Octopus + OctopusTiles 4 + 7 · Pair found · 4 / 4

The result is 6 moves, 4 / 4 pairs, completed. Both mismatches remain in your record. Finding the pairs in four moves is also a complete circuit; this exercise includes two useful mistakes.

Keep a completed circuit

After the fourth pair, choose Keep this circuit. The Kept for later area shows your practice name and final moves after the record has been stored.

One completed circuit can be kept in this browser. If a circuit is already kept, the page asks before replacing it. Choose Replace kept circuit to replace it, or Cancel to keep the existing record.

Choose Download record for a text copy with every attempt, all pair names, tile IDs, final moves and completion. Downloading does not change the kept record.

Unkept current play resets on reload. A kept circuit stays in this browser’s local storage until you forget it or the browser clears its site data. Keeping does not send the record to another device.

Practice again without losing kept work

Restart this circuit turns the current board face down and clears its attempts. It uses the same tile arrangement and practice name.

New practice starts another circuit with a different, reproducible tile arrangement. It keeps the current practice name; use Rename this practice, enter a name of up to 120 characters, and choose Use this name to change it.

Both actions leave the deliberately kept record intact. Keep a completed circuit before starting again if you want to preserve those turns.

Recover or forget a kept circuit

Choose Recover kept circuit to read the completed circuit and all its attempts again. If current play has started, the page asks before replacing that current play. The kept copy stays available.

Choose Forget kept circuit, then confirm Forget this kept circuit to remove the stored copy. The circuit currently on screen remains visible. Choose Cancel to leave the kept copy intact.

If the kept record cannot be read, the page tells you and leaves it stored. You can explicitly forget it or replace it with another completed circuit. If this browser blocks local storage, you can still play and download a completed record.

Use the keyboard

Press Tab to move between links, controls and available tiles. Press Enter or Space on a tile button to turn it. A visible outline shows your focus.

Face-up tiles keep their names. Matched tiles are removed from the focus sequence. After a match, focus moves to an available tile; after the final pair, it moves to the completion section. Mismatches remain visible until you activate their dismissal button.

Controls use native browser buttons and the current turn has a live text status. Pictures have nearby text names. Reduced-motion settings remove the tile lift transition.

Ready for a little connection?

Begin with eight tiles. Take your time, keep a circuit you want to remember, and come back for another.
