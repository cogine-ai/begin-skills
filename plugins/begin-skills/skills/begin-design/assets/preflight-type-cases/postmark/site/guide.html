01 · Write and inspect

Open the maker with its complete thank-you example, or choose a paper size from the home page. Name your postcard, enter the recipient and sender, and write your message. Every word remains editable.

The physical composition uses the exact landscape paper proportions. On a phone its small type can be difficult to inspect, so All current words gives you a complete readable copy. Meaningful manual line breaks remain in both the postcard and text file.

If your current words do not fit, the physical preview shows the earlier valid composition and says so. The editor and current reading copy still show your new words. Shorten the indicated content or try the larger paper.

02 · Give the words room

The maker uses fixed printed type sizes. It does not clip your message or make it progressively smaller to squeeze in more words.

  • The message allows 420 characters, including spaces and line breaks. Blank lines use printed room too.
  • The postcard name, recipient and sender each allow 48 characters. Recipient and sender must also fit on one printed line at the chosen fixed size.
  • Supported Latin letters, accents and punctuation can be printed. Unsupported characters, including emoji and tabs, receive a correction before preparation.
  • Long words need to fit the paper width. Add a natural space or shorten a word if the maker reports that it is too wide.

A character count alone does not promise a fit: wide letters and extra line breaks can fill the print area sooner. The visible correction tells you the limiting condition. Input stays available while you correct it.

03 · Prepare, keep and recover

  1. Choose the postcard name before preparation. It labels your browser copy and download files.
  2. Select Prepare postcard. The prepared copy contains exactly those words and that paper size.
  3. Read the prepared words and composition. Choose Keep named postcard to retain one copy on this browser, or download either file.
  4. Later, choose Recover kept postcard to restore the kept words to the editor and prepared copy. Recovery replaces the open draft; it does not merge messages.

After any edit, the earlier prepared copy stays visible. If the current words differ, its keep and download buttons are blocked until you prepare again. Invalid edits also block those actions. Returning to exactly the prepared words makes that same copy current again.

Keeping a second postcard replaces the first browser copy. Forget kept postcard removes that stored copy, while the words and prepared artifact currently open stay on the page. Downloaded files stay wherever you put them.

04 · Know your files

The SVG is the printable composition. It declares the physical width and height in millimetres, preserves the full message in visible text and its embedded record, and includes the original Sun after rain artwork. Unchanged Newsreader roman and italic and Work Sans fonts are embedded with their complete license notices.

A modern browser that honors embedded TTF fonts and SVG text is the intended viewing route. Some design applications ignore embedded web fonts and can change the line fit through substitution. Inspect the downloaded SVG before printing or importing it elsewhere.

The TXT is the complete reading record. It includes your postcard name, paper size, dimensions, recipient, full message with manual line breaks, sender and a print note. It is an editable text file, not a page-layout file.

The prepared panel also lets you inspect the complete text file before downloading. Neither file includes an address side, postal service or bleed area.

05 · Put it on paper

  1. Download the current printable SVG and open it in a modern browser. Inspect the complete message, accented names and both paper edges.
  2. In the print dialog, use 100% or actual size, turn off fit-to-page scaling and browser headers/footers, and choose landscape orientation. If you print on a larger sheet, keep the card at its stated size.
  3. Print a plain-paper test. Measure the card with a ruler: A6 is 148 × 105 mm, and A5 is 210 × 148 mm. Correct any driver scaling before using your chosen stock.
  4. Use paper your printer supports. Trim the larger sheet to the declared card size, then tuck the note into a gift or hand it over.

The illustration and cream background are part of the printable composition. Printer color, margins and stock can change the result. Physical printer output has not been verified in this local trial.

If your printer cannot keep the intended size from the browser, place the SVG on a document page at its exact dimensions in an application that preserves its embedded fonts, inspect it again, then print the test.

06 · Your browser copy

Your input, preview and files are made locally in this browser. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed. There are no postal, payment or address-collection services.

Preparation alone does not keep work after closing the page. Deliberate keeping stores one named postcard in this site's browser storage. The same browser profile and local site address are needed to recover it. Private browsing, cleared site data or browser storage limits can remove or prevent that copy.

If keeping fails, the prepared copy stays open and the message tells you. Download its files to retain it yourself. A damaged browser record can be forgotten; it is never silently inserted into the editor.
