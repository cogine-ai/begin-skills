The evening guide

Prepare a card, read the midnight clock, and manage your browser copy. The schedule is fictional.

01 · Prepare a query

  1. Choose a departure stop in From and a different arrival stop in To. There are four stops in this demonstration.
  2. Enter the earliest time you can leave in Earliest departure. Use a two-digit hour and minute, such as 18:00 or 23:40.
  3. Choose Prepare travel card. Read the query printed above the routes; it identifies the exact inputs those cards describe.

The planner opens with North Quay → São Bento Gardens at 23:40. This default is already prepared. It has three options: 00:24, 00:35 and 01:25 next day arrivals.

Editing any input marks the previous cards stale. They remain visible for reference. An invalid input keeps the previous complete result and shows an error beside the field. Keeping and downloading stay blocked until you prepare a valid current query, even if you change back to the old values.

02 · Read the midnight clock

The single demonstration evening is 17 October 2030. Its clock runs from 18:00 that day through 01:30 on 18 October. Every time is local to the fictional city; there are no time zones or live clock calculations.

23:50 is late on the demonstration evening.

00:10 next day is twenty minutes later.

01:25 next day is eighty-five minutes after midnight.

The model stores absolute local minutes from 1080 (18:00) through 1530 (25:30). Midnight is minute 1440. This lets a next-day arrival sort after an evening departure. The display uses the familiar clock with next day spelled out.

In the input, 00:00–01:30 always refers to the next day. Explicit 24:00–25:30 is accepted too. For example, 24:10 and 00:10 mean the same moment. Times outside this evening, incomplete formats and minutes above 59 are rejected.

03 · Compare complete routes

Options are ranked by actual arrival time, then fewer transfers, then earlier first boarding. The first option can require a transfer when it arrives sooner than a direct journey. Ranking is a transparent schedule comparison, not a recommendation for real travel.

Every leg states its service, boarding stop and time, arrival stop and time, waiting before boarding and travel duration. The initial wait starts at the query’s earliest departure. A later leg’s wait is the transfer time after the previous arrival.

Journey duration includes all waiting and time on board from your earliest departure to the final arrival. The card also totals those two parts separately. Open Scheduled calls on a leg to read every stop that service passes between boarding and arrival.

A transfer must allow at least five minutes. The planner enumerates valid journeys from the complete schedule without repeating a stop or trip. Some later options include long waits. It shows them fully instead of concealing the waiting time.

A valid query with no route replaces the previous result with an explicit unavailable state. It cannot produce a travel card to keep. Check the full schedule below, or prepare a different query.

04 · Keep, recover and forget

  1. Use Selected card above the results, or the radio labelled Select this card on a current option. The selected card is visibly marked. Read selected card details jumps to its complete facts; Back to card controls returns to keeping and download. Selection does not change the displayed order.
  2. Use Keep card in this browser beside the prepared query to store the full prepared query, all options and your selected card in local browser storage. It is kept on this browser and origin only. There is no account, cloud sync or automatic saving.
  3. After a reload, the planner opens its default query and tells you whether a kept card is available. Choose Recover kept card to deliberately restore it. The current fixture is used to verify the saved records before recovery.
  4. Choose Forget kept card to remove only this planner’s saved browser record. The current on-screen cards stay available. A corrupt or older saved record cannot be recovered; forgetting it removes that copy.

Private browsing or browser settings may prevent local keeping. A failed keep reports the failure and leaves the current result intact. Downloading remains an available way to retain a valid current card.

Download travel card (.txt) creates a UTF-8 text file. It includes the demonstration date, full query, every prepared option, selected-card identity, leg services, boarding and arrival stops/times, every scheduled call, waiting and travel durations, transfer counts and limits. Keep this file wherever you choose. Forgetting a browser card does not delete an exported file.

06 · What this card can tell you

A card describes a valid journey in this fixed fictional schedule. It does not describe a real transport service. There are no real fares, payments, bookings, location requests, traffic estimates, live providers or accessibility claims.

The five-minute transfer is a demonstration rule. It is not evidence that a real interchange is usable, safe or accessible. The schedule is not a transport recommendation, and a downloaded or kept card cannot make a booking.
