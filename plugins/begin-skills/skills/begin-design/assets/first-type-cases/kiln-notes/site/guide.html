Prepare an Ash no. 03 worksheet and compare its four weights with the sheet below. All work stays in your browser.

Keep a copy you can return to

When the sheet is current, select Keep in this browser. This replaces the one previously kept copy with your prepared recipe, batch, weights and note.

Reload the worksheet. The kept copy and its inputs return together. An unkept preparation or field edit does not survive reload.

Restore kept copy discards the draft edits and restores the last kept worksheet. Use it when you want to return to that version without reloading.

Keeping depends on this browser’s local storage. If storage is blocked, the page reports the failure and leaves your current preparation available. You can download its text to keep a separate file. Clearing browser data can remove the kept copy.

Edit and prepare again

After preparing a worksheet, change the batch, formula or note. The earlier result stays visible and its status begins with Stale. It still shows the values and note from the previous preparation.

Keep in this browser and Download text are disabled while the result is stale. Select Prepare worksheet again to attach your current inputs to a new result.

Try changing Ash no. 03 from 250 to 500 g. After preparation, Feldspar becomes 200 g, Silica 150 g, Whiting 100 g and Kaolin 50 g, for a 500 g total.

Changing an input back to its earlier value still requires a fresh preparation. A kept copy remains separate until you explicitly keep the new current sheet.

Correct an entry

Try 250.5 in Desired dry batch and select Prepare worksheet. The entry stays editable, the field asks for whole grams, and the previous result stays visible as stale.

Change the entry to 250 and prepare again. The error clears and the new worksheet becomes current.

Start a fresh worksheet

Reset worksheet clears the draft note and prepared result, then returns the inputs to Ash no. 03 at 250 g. Your kept copy stays available for restoration.

Forget kept copy removes the saved copy from this browser. The current inputs and visible preparation stay in place. Downloaded files remain on your device after either action.

Read the proportions

Each base formula totals 100 parts. The worksheet multiplies each material’s parts by the desired dry batch and divides by 100.

For Ash at 250 g, Feldspar is 40 × 250 ÷ 100 = 100 g. For Clay at 125 g, Silica is 35 × 125 ÷ 100 = 43.75 g.

Weights retain up to two decimal places and omit unnecessary zeroes. Because the requested batch is a whole number of grams and each part is a whole percentage, this preserves the calculation’s useful precision.

These are fictional teaching entries. The worksheet does not establish how a material behaves in a kiln, provide a firing schedule or offer a tested glaze recipe.
