01 / Prepare the example

A room heater for the month

Start with Room heater and prepare a sheet for 1500 watts, 4 hours each day, 30 days and 25 cents per kWh. It should show 180 kWh and USD 45.00.

Open the room heater example ↗

This link fills the example inputs. A kept sheet remains available through Restore kept sheet. You can begin without resetting.

  1. Check Appliance name is Room heater. Set Power to 1500 and Daily use to 4.
  2. Set Days in the month to 30 and Electricity rate to 25. The rate is in cents for each kWh.
  3. Optionally add “Four hours each evening” in A note for your sheet. Choose Prepare energy sheet.

The sheet’s status becomes Current. Compare the labels and values below. Your note appears below the working and in the downloaded text.

02 / Check the working

Follow the numbers

Power × daily hours × days gives watt-hours. Dividing by 1000 gives kilowatt-hours, or kWh. Multiply kWh by the entered cents rate, then divide by 100 to get dollars.

Energy1500 × 4 × 30 ÷ 1000 = 180 kWh

Cost180 × 25 ÷ 100 = USD 45.00

Cost is rounded to the nearest cent for display. The calculation uses whole-number watt-hours and cents, so it does not accumulate floating-point money errors. At exactly half a cent, it rounds up.

03 / Edit and correct

Keep an old sheet in its place

After preparing the example, change Daily use from 4 to 5. The earlier prepared sheet stays at 180 kWh and USD 45.00, and its status becomes Stale. Keep and Download text become unavailable.

Choose Prepare energy sheet again. The new current version shows 225 kWh and USD 56.25. Editing a name or note also makes the earlier sheet stale; even returning an input to its previous value requires preparing again.

Try a correction

Enter 4.5 in Daily use and prepare. You should see a whole-number error beside that field. Your entry and previous prepared sheet remain. Change it to 4 and prepare again to return to 180 kWh and USD 45.00.

The worksheet accepts whole digits only. Blank fields, commas, decimals and values beyond the stated range need a correction. Appliance name is required; the note is optional.

04 / Keep and recover

Current and kept mean different things

Current means the prepared sheet matches the inputs since the last preparation. Kept means you explicitly saved a copy in this browser. Preparing alone does not keep anything.

  1. With the room heater sheet Current, choose Keep in this browser. The kept message names its version, appliance and total.
  2. Reload the worksheet page. The kept inputs, note and prepared sheet return. The status is Current.
  3. Edit Daily use to 5 without keeping it. Choose Restore kept sheet to recover the previously kept inputs and prepared result.

Keep replaces the one earlier kept copy. Another preparation does not replace it. Unsaved work disappears on reload. If browser storage is unavailable, use Download text to retain a copy.

A damaged kept record is not loaded. The worksheet tells you that recovery failed; reset lets you remove that record and start again.

06 / Limits and boundaries

What this sheet can tell you

This is an illustrative constant-power estimate from the values entered. Actual appliance power can change during use. The worksheet contains no actual tariff, metered data, energy advice, utility integration or payment.

Check the upper boundary

Enter 10000 watts, 24 daily hours, 31 days and 100 cents per kWh. Prepare again. Expected Monthly energy: 7440 kWh and Illustrative monthly cost: USD 7440.00.

Inputs, keeping and downloads work locally. Nothing is sent to a server. The browser holds one kept sheet under this website’s storage key; clearing browser data removes it.
