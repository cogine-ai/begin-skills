## Local font

`assets/fonts/fieldnotes-sans.woff2` is **Fieldnotes Sans**, the renamed Source Sans 3 variable-font subset already present in the frozen portable input. It was copied unchanged from `begin-design/assets/composition-cases/proofnote/site/fonts/fieldnotes-sans.woff2`. Its complete SIL Open Font License 1.1 notice is retained unchanged at `assets/fonts/source-sans-3-OFL.txt`.

The recorded original derivation used fontTools 4.66.1 and a Google Fonts variable TTF, with coverage `U+0000-00FF,U+0100-024F,U+2000-206F,U+20AC,U+2190-21FF`. The derivative was renamed **Fieldnotes Sans** to respect the reserved original name **Source**. Cadence retains that internal and CSS identity and made no font changes. The full font notice must accompany redistribution.
