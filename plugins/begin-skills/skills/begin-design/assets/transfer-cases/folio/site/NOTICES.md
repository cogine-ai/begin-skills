# Folio Press notices

Folio Press is a fictional independent publisher. All six book identities, author names, descriptions, geometric cover artwork, favicon, page compositions, source and local sample interactions were authored for this transfer study. They are concept material; they do not represent available publications, real author endorsements or actual orders. Project-authored material retains Begin's current licensing status; this notice does not grant additional redistribution rights.

The revised edition adds six substantial original interior text samples and two original drawing studies. Soft Geometry's drawing studies the seams and softened base of a paper bag; Lines Between Places compares two notebook routes through an imagined place. The essay samples are original prose and exercises. The two photography titles include explicitly labeled original photographer’s field notes, with no photographic plates included in this concept sample. They do not represent real photographs, real commissioned books or third-party artworks. Their page references and author voices belong to these imagined book identities.
