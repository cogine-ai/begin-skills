Build this two-book bag

If you already have books in your bag, keep them or export them first. For the exact exercise below, remove those lines or use Clear bag. Clearing changes only your sample selection.

  1. Add Soft Geometry

    Open Soft Geometry. Set Format to Paperback — $36.00 and Quantity to 2. Choose Add to sample bag.

    Your bag shows Soft Geometry · Paperback, 2 copies and $72.00.

  2. Add The Room Next Door

    Use Choose a book to select The Room Next Door. Set Format to Hardback — $42.00 and Quantity to 1. Choose Add to sample bag.

    The new line is The Room Next Door · Hardback, 1 copy and $42.00.

  3. Check the current selection

    Go to Your sample bag. You should have two lines, three copies and a total of $114.00.

Keep it and return

Each successful addition, update or removal keeps the bag automatically. Reload the page in the same browser: the two lines and the $114.00 total should return. You can visit the catalogue and come back through Sample bag.

The selection belongs to this browser and this website address. Another browser, device or address has its own bag. If your browser prevents saving, the page tells you; export your current bag before leaving.

Correct a selection

  1. Change a quantity

    In Soft Geometry's bag line, set Quantity to 1, then choose that line's Update. The bag should show 2 copies and $78.00. Set it back to 2 and update to restore $114.00.

  2. Change a format

    In The Room Next Door's bag line, set Format to Paperback, then choose Update. With two Soft Geometry paperbacks, the new total is $102.00. Set the line back to Hardback and update to restore $114.00.

  3. Remove a book

    Choose Remove beside The Room Next Door. Soft Geometry remains: 2 copies, $72.00. You can select The Room Next Door again and add one hardback to restore the exercise.

  4. Recover from an invalid quantity

    Try a quantity of 9 or 1.5, then choose Update. An error asks for a whole quantity from 1 to 8. The entered value stays visible and the bag total stays unchanged. Correct it and update again.

Paperback and hardback are separate bag lines. Adding the same book and format again adds to that line. Changing into a format already present combines the quantities; a combined quantity above 8 is rejected.

Take the list with you

Choose Export current bag. A file named folio-press-sample-bag.txt downloads, and Current text export appears below the controls. For the original exercise it includes both formats, 3 copies and Total: $114.00.

After any successful bag change, the visible export text updates too. Choose Export current bag again to download the changed list.

To remove all selections, choose Clear bag. Your bag returns to 0 copies and $0.00; reloading keeps it empty. Clearing is optional. Export a list first if you want to retain it.

What the bag includes

Prices
All amounts are USD. The displayed paperback prices are fixed; every hardback costs $12.00 more. The sample totals contain book prices only, with no shipping or tax calculation.
Quantities
Each book and format combination has 1–8 whole copies. An invalid update leaves the previously kept bag unchanged.
Saving
Successful changes keep automatically in this browser. No account or shared bag is provided. A blocked or unreadable saved bag produces a visible message.
Outcome
This is a fictional catalogue and a local sample selection. Nothing is ordered, sent to the publisher or charged.
