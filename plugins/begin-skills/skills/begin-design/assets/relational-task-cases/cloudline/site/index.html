01

One day carries the peak

On 4 October, the measure reaches 14.4 mm. It is the largest reading in this week, and makes up just over two fifths of the total. Averaging all seven days softens that single high point into 5.09 mm per day.

02

Zero is still an observation

The readings on 2 and 6 October are both 0.0 mm. They belong in the record. Leaving a field empty would mean a missing reading; entering zero means a reading was made and recorded as zero.

03

A line we choose to compare

The ochre line sits at 5.0 mm. Three days lie strictly above it: 3, 4 and 7 October. The line is a choice for this exercise. It changes the comparison, not the measurements, total or daily mean.
