Cloudline's brand, text, graph, icon, pages and implementation are original fictional educational material created for this task. No photographs or outside illustrations are used. The rainfall readings are invented, supplied teaching values.

Local font bytes are unchanged from the official Google Fonts repository. Their exact source URLs, byte counts and SHA-256 identities are in `FONT_SOURCES.json`.

All complete notices remain with the fonts. No font data was converted, subsetted or renamed internally.
