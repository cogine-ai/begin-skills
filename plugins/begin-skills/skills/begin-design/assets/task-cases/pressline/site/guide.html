04 / SAVE, RELOAD AND RESET

Keep the sample between visits

When browser storage is available, saved jobs, filters and the open record resume after a reload on the same website address and browser profile. Unsaved drafts and checkbox selections do not survive reload.

  1. Reload after saving P-1049

    Its saved values remain Scheduled, 19 October and 5.5 hours. The totals remain 10 active, 5 due, 1 blocked and 37.5 hours.

  2. Choose Reset sample to repeat

    This replaces the local sample with all twelve original jobs, clears search and filters, clears checked jobs and opens P-1049. Its original values return: Blocked, 14 October and 7 hours.

If saving is unavailable

The desk reports that changes last for the current page only. A reload then restores the originals. Use sample information: this desk has no accounts, payments, shared staff access or connection to a real studio.

05 / DESK REFERENCE

Read the view correctly

Search
Matches part of an ID, job title or client name, without case sensitivity. Try Oriel, programs or P-1049.
Status and Owner
Combine with search. Owner identifies the staff member responsible for the job. Clients commission print work.
Reset filters
Clears search, Status and Owner. It leaves saved jobs and the open record intact.
Selected count
Counts checked jobs in the visible queue. The header checkbox selects every visible job. Filter changes clear all checks.
Open record
Shows the job whose title you opened. It can remain open outside the active filters, with a notice explaining why.
Studio totals
Always use all twelve saved jobs. Active excludes Ready for delivery. Due by 15 Oct counts active jobs due from 11–15 October inclusive. Estimated hours sums every job, including ready jobs.
Group totals
Count and sum only matching jobs in that status group. The view footer sums estimated hours for all matching jobs.
Empty results
Search zinc to try a zero-result view. Show all jobs clears the filters and restores the complete queue.
Editable fields
Status must be one of the four listed states. Due date must be a valid calendar date. Estimated hours must be a finite number of zero or more; decimal hours are accepted.
