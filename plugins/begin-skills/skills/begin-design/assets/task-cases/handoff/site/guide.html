Prepare a useful brief

Open Prepare a review brief. You need JavaScript and four details about one document or support task. Use fictional or non-sensitive information in this example.

  1. Choose the scope

    Guide review examines an existing guide and produces annotations, open questions, and revised instructions for an agreed section. Support handoff organizes one diagnostic task and produces a procedure, an escalation checklist, and open decisions.

  2. Name the audience and date

    In Audience, say who will use the document and what they know. Enter at least 8 characters and 2 words. Select today or a future Intended handoff date. A target date does not confirm delivery.

  3. Describe the current problem

    Name the unclear step and its effect on the reader. Enter at least 24 characters and 5 words. The brief retains your wording, with leading and trailing spaces removed.

  4. Prepare and check the text

    Select Prepare a review brief. The result shows your scope, audience, intended date, and problem, followed by the scope's proposed outputs. Check the Ready to use status and read the whole text before copying or downloading.

HANDOFF OFFICE — REVIEW BRIEF
Fictional service example; prepared locally, not sent.

Scope: Guide review (document review)
Audience: First-time users setting up the USB sensor
Intended handoff date: 6 November 2026

Current problem:
The setup guide says to wait for the sensor to be ready, but it never names the status light or explains when to stop retrying.

Proposed outputs:
- Annotated draft
- Questions for the product team to resolve
- Revised instructions for the agreed section

To agree before work begins:
The document or task, the boundaries of the review, the delivery date, and who will confirm product behavior.

This brief does not confirm a booking or delivery date.

This output uses the four values above. If you choose another future date, the date in your prepared brief will change.

Save and return

Select Save locally to keep both the current input and the last prepared brief in this browser. Reloading this site restores the most recent local save. Changes are not saved automatically; the label changes to Changes not saved locally after an edit.

Local storage belongs to this browser and this site's address. Another browser, private window, or different local port may have a different save. Clearing browser data can remove it. If storage is unavailable, the page tells you and keeps the current work on screen.

For a brief with Ready to use status, use Copy brief or Download .txt. The download is a UTF-8 text file. If clipboard access fails, download the file or select the prepared text manually.

These actions prepare or retain text on your device. Handoff Office receives no message, attachment, or booking.

Correct or reset

An edit makes the prepared brief out of date

The prepared panel keeps its original text so you can see the previous basis. Changing a form value shows Out of date and disables copying and downloading. Select Prepare a review brief again to replace it with your current input. Returning all fields to the original values restores Ready to use.

A required detail is missing

Follow the error beside the field. Your input stays in place, and the previous prepared text remains available to inspect. Fix the field and prepare again.

Start over

Select Reset, then Clear this brief to remove the current input, prepared text, and local save. This cannot recover the cleared values. Choose Keep working to cancel.

Contact after a real review

The demo@example.invalid link opens an email app. It is an unusable example address. A real service needs its own contact destination and agreed scope before documents are shared.
