Open the workshop programme ↗

  1. Choose “Print a little poster”

    In Choose a workshop, select the illustrated poster card. You can use Making to narrow the list. The workshop details should say 2 hours, Beginner, and $36 per person.

  2. Choose 28 November at 14:00

    Under Workshop date, choose Saturday 28 November 2026 · 14:00. Each workshop has its own two dates. Changing workshops clears a date that the new workshop does not offer.

  3. Set “People” to 2

    Enter 2. The current choice now shows 2 people, $36 × 2, and a $72 total. People must be a whole number from 1 to 4.

  4. Check, then save

    Choose Save local plan. The separate Your saved plan receipt should show the workshop, the 28 November date, 2 people and $72. The current choice reads Matches saved plan.

02 / Change your mind

Edit, return or reload

Make an unsaved edit

After saving the example, change People to 3. Your current total becomes $108, and the current choice says Unsaved changes. The saved receipt still shows 2 people and $72.

Choose Save local plan again only when you want to replace that saved receipt with the new choice. There is one saved plan at a time.

Return without replacing it

Choose Return to saved choice in the saved receipt. The form restores the saved workshop, date and people, and the filter returns to All. In our example, it restores 2 people and $72.

Reload the saved choice

If you saved the two-person example and edited it without saving again, reload the programme page. When browser storage is available, the saved two-person choice loads into the form. The unsaved three-person edit is discarded.

Filtering changes the workshop list. If your current workshop is outside the chosen category, the first workshop in that category becomes your current choice. Your saved receipt stays as it was.

03 / A fresh start

Reset your plan

Choose Reset plan and choices after the saved-plan receipt. It clears the local saved plan and sets the current choice to Clay basics, no date, 1 person, and the All filter. The current cost is $48, and a date is still required before saving.

After a successful reset, reloading returns to those same initial choices. If browser storage cannot be cleared, a warning explains that an older saved plan may return after reload.

04 / The practical bits

Save in this browser

Your plan uses this browser’s local storage. It is tied to this site address and browser profile. It does not follow you to another device, browser or site address. Private browsing, clearing site data, or storage restrictions can remove it.

If storage is unavailable, the page keeps the plan for the current open tab and labels it Kept in this tab only. A warning says that reloading may lose it. Keep your own note if you need it later.

I can’t save my choice +

Choose an offered date and enter a whole number from 1 to 4 in People. After an invalid save, the first field that needs attention receives focus and has a specific message. Your entered choice stays in place so you can correct it.

My saved plan is missing +

Check that you are using the same browser, profile and site address. A different address has separate storage. If the stored record is unreadable or no longer matches the programme, the planner shows a warning and starts with Clay basics. You can make and save a new plan.

What happens when I save? +

The browser keeps the workshop, date and number of people. The fee and total come from the programme. No account, email, network delivery or payment is involved. This fictional studio has no real venue or reserved seats.
