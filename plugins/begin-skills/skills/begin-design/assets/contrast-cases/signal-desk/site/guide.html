02 / EPISODE EDITOR

Put Avery's Tuesday edit on Monday

  1. Open A room full of borrowed voices

    Choose its title in the queue. The editor must show SD-03 and People & memory. On phone, the title takes you to the full-width editor; Back to episode queue returns to the list.

  2. Choose Monday and Morning

    Leave Editor at Avery and Estimated edit at 180. These are draft values. The queue still shows Tuesday afternoon until you choose Apply to queue.

  3. Choose Apply to queue

    The row moves to Monday morning, queue revision becomes 01, and the whole-queue total stays 840 minutes. If a filter hides this episode, the editor names that condition; Show all makes its row visible again.

03 / CLASH AND CAPACITY

Two checks name the problem

Slot clash: SD-01 + SD-03 share Avery's Monday morning slot.

Daily overload: Avery has 330 minutes on Monday: 90 over the 240-minute daily limit.

A morning and an afternoon assignment can still overload the day. Changing only SD-03 to Monday afternoon clears the slot clash, but Avery still has 330 minutes. Both checks must pass before preparation.

  1. Repair the open SD-03 record

    Set Day to Tuesday 13 Oct, Slot to Afternoon and Estimated edit to 150. Keep Avery as editor. Choose Apply to queue.

  2. Compare the new totals

    Queue revision is now 02. Six episodes total 810 minutes. Avery's Monday is 150 minutes; Tuesday is 150. The checks say All assignments fit and no issues remain.

05 / LOCAL KEEP AND RECOVERY

Keep one useful version

  1. Choose Keep locally after the 810-minute plan

    This explicitly stores the current prepared text and all six queue records in this browser. The local status changes to Local copy exists. Edits are not saved automatically.

  2. Make an unkept change

    Open SD-06, Instructions for a city that never sleeps. Change Estimated edit from 165 to 180 and choose Apply to queue. The queue is revision 03 and totals 825 minutes. The previous 810-minute plan becomes Stale, so it cannot be kept or downloaded.

  3. Choose Reload kept plan

    This discards the open draft and unkept queue changes, restoring revision 02, SD-06 at 165 minutes and the Current 810-minute plan. Reloading the weekly queue page also loads that kept copy. The overview reads the kept copy when it opens; reload the overview to see a later kept version.

Forget local copy
Removes the kept browser copy. It leaves your current open queue and prepared text in place. You may still download a current plan or choose Keep locally again. Reloading the queue page without a kept copy starts from the original 840-minute week.
Restore example week
Replaces the open queue with the original six records, revision 00 and no prepared text. It does not remove a kept copy. For a permanent return to the original example, choose Forget local copy, then Restore example week.
Storage unavailable
If the browser blocks local storage, Keep locally and local recovery are unavailable. You can still edit, prepare and download during the open session. Download before closing the page if you want to retain the text.
Unrecognized local copy
The original week opens if a saved copy does not match this example's record format. A message explains the condition. Forget local copy removes the unreadable copy; it is not silently replaced.

The local copy belongs to this browser profile and serving address. Another browser, device or address has a separate copy. A text download can be read elsewhere, but this example has no import or shared team plan.

06 / PLANNING RULES

What the checks mean

Editor
Avery, Jules or Noor is the fictional staff member assigned to edit the story. The producer is the person arranging the week. Listeners are the audience for the finished documentary.
Day and slot
The fixed week has five days and two work blocks per day. One editor can have one episode in a morning slot and one in an afternoon slot. These are planning blocks, not exact start times or confirmed editor availability.
Edit minutes
The producer's estimate of remaining editing work. Every estimate is 15–240 minutes in steps of 15. Each editor can have at most 240 minutes in one day, across both slots. Different editors may work in the same slot.
Selected record
Opening a title inspects that episode. If you have unapplied edits, apply or cancel them before opening another title. The Day and Editor filters change the visible queue, while the selected episode remains available in its editor.
Keyboard
Use Tab to reach title buttons, fields and actions. Enter opens a focused episode or applies its form. Arrow keys change native selections and the estimate in 15-minute steps. There is no drag-only operation.
Prepared and kept
Prepared is a snapshot of an issue-free queue revision. Kept is one current snapshot explicitly retained locally. The six fictional records do not connect to real audio files, schedules, accounts or team messaging.
