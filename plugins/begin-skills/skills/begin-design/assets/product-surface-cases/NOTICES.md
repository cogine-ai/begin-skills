# Product surface cases: notices

LIFT, BENCHTIME, and CUTLINE are original fictional examples created for Begin's product-surface study. Their copy, interface, CSS, diagrams and original artwork are concept material. They do not represent actual vehicle engineering, safety approval, machine availability, reservations, purchases, external submissions or customer endorsements.

Native screenshots are copied without transformation from the source-only local study. Selected source retains the inspected refinement, with two later input-identity guards in LIFT and CUTLINE recorded in separate release-review freezes. Those guards do not alter the captured layout; the screenshots were not rerendered after that repair. Earlier first sources, font comparisons, raw research and review artifacts remain outside this collection. Screenshots document the visible captured page and do not independently prove operation or a completed external action.
