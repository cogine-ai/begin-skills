Add the modules you need and watch your LIFT take shape. The total always includes the frame and every selected module.

03

Take your plans with you

Give your setup a name, check the itemised price, then select Prepare configuration. Your complete setup appears just below, ready to download.

Download configuration sheet gives you a self-contained HTML file with the bike, selected parts, quantities, and price breakdown. Open it in a browser or print it. Export complete JSON gives you the same configuration as structured data.

Use Save a local draft to keep a checkpoint. Restore saved draft brings that checkpoint back, replacing your current setup. Working changes are also kept automatically so you can visit this guide and return.

Storage belongs to this browser and device. Private browsing, clearing site data, or blocked storage can remove or prevent drafts. Download a sheet or JSON to keep a separate copy. A storage problem is shown in the studio.

What does Reset configuration change?

Reset returns the current setup to a Porter with no added modules and the name “My everyday setup”. Your separate saved draft remains available to restore.

Have I ordered a bike?

No. LIFT is a fictional product exploration. “Prepared locally” means a configuration file was created in your browser. No payment is taken, nothing is sent to a seller, and no order or reservation exists. Prices are illustrative, not an offer for sale.

Can I change a prepared setup?

Yes. Change any frame, module, or name above the result. The old prepared result closes so you can prepare a fresh sheet with the current values. Your downloaded files keep the values they had when exported.
