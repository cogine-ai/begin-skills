One evening at Table Nine

The good part
takes its time

Pull up a chair. We’ll start with something sharp and bright, pass warm plates across the table, and finish when the candles have burned a little lower.

Table Nine is an independent supper-club concept with two tables of four. The menu follows a fictional October kitchen: roots, orchard fruit, good bread, and a generous pot at the centre.

This invitation and its place are fictional. The site is an illustrative project; it does not take reservations or payments.

On the table

A sample four-course evening

  1. 01

    Roasted beetroot, apple, bitter leaves and a mustard dressing.

    Bright / crisp
  2. 02

    Pass / share
  3. 03

  4. 04

    Sweet / slow
