01 / Make the service record

Prepare the eight-guest fixture

Open the seating desk. It starts with all eight fictional guests placed at North and South. You can prepare this starting arrangement immediately; no account or external service is needed.

  1. Read the Evening title and Service note. Keep the starting text or enter your own. A title is required; a note can be empty.
  2. Check each guest’s Table and Seat. North and South each have seats 1–4. No seat can be shared. Aida Moreno and Noor Rahman must stay at the same table.
  3. Select Prepare seating record. The prepared record appears below the action with the complete title, all guest names and IDs, dietary notes, seats, table totals and service note.
  4. Read the full record. Select Inspect the exact text if you want to see the download’s contents, then Download exact text. Your browser receives table-nine-seating.txt in UTF-8; check its Downloads area.

02 / Make a change

Correct a seating conflict

After preparing the fixture, change Celia Park’s seat from 3 to 4. North seat 4 now has Celia and Lucian. The table view shows both IDs. The earlier prepared record remains visible and is labelled Earlier record · inputs changed.

Select Prepare seating record. Preparation is blocked and the error links to Celia’s seat. Download and Keep are unavailable while the current choices are invalid or differ from the prepared record.

To swap their places, change Lucian’s seat to 3. Now Celia is at North seat 4 and Lucian is at North seat 3. Prepare again. The new record lists them in seat order and becomes available for downloading or keeping.

Leaving any Table or Seat unassigned, putting five people at one table, or moving only Aida or Noor to the other table also blocks preparation. The desk preserves the earlier output so you can compare it while correcting the inputs.

Three versions to recognize

Current inputs are your editable choices. A prepared record captures the choices at the last successful preparation. A kept copy is the prepared version you deliberately saved in this browser. Editing one does not silently update the others.

03 / Continue later

Keep and recover the same version

If the prepared record matches the valid current inputs, select Keep prepared record. A successful message means this browser accepted the local copy. Keeping replaces this desk’s previously kept copy; it does not create a second version.

Reload the seating desk page. The fixture opens, and the Keep it here section offers Recover kept copy. The kept copy is never applied automatically. Recover only when you want its inputs to replace the current inputs.

Recovery restores the full title, note, every guest assignment and the exact prepared output together. Your recovered record is ready to review and download. You can edit it, prepare a new version, and deliberately keep that version when you are ready.

If storage is blocked or full, the desk explains that keeping failed and retains your current work. Download the valid prepared record instead. If a kept copy is unreadable, recovery leaves current inputs and earlier output unchanged. You can forget that unreadable copy.

04 / Start again

Restore inputs or forget the kept copy

Restore fixture puts the title, note and eight guest places back to the starting arrangement. The earlier prepared result may remain visible; prepare again if it differs from the restored inputs. Restoring does not remove a kept copy.

Forget kept copy removes only the Table Nine record from this browser’s local storage. It leaves current inputs, prepared output, downloaded files and other websites’ data intact. After forgetting, reloading opens the fixture without offering that previous copy.

A kept copy belongs to the browser and site address where you made it. Another device, another browser or a different local address may not have it. Browser cleanup can remove it, so download a file if you need a separate record.

05 / Know the limits

A local planning illustration

The desk runs in the browser with eight fixed fictional guests. Their names, IDs and dietary notes are retained; only the title, service note, table and seat choices are editable. Nothing is sent to a host, cook or guest. The site does not book a dinner, accept payment or provide a backend guest database.

Dietary notes are supplied sample information. “None” means no dietary note is recorded in the fixture; it is not confirmation that a guest has no restrictions. Confirm needs directly and discuss ingredients and preparation before real service. Neither the note nor this sample menu is a medical or allergen-safety guarantee.

You can open the static pages locally or serve the folder from a local web address. JavaScript is needed for the desk. If a browser restricts storage for local files, preparation and download remain available; keeping and recovery may be unavailable.
