# OVERPRINT notices

OVERPRINT is a fictional small print studio. All website wording, atlas drawings and layout are original material made for this exercise. The atlas is a self-initiated concept, not client work. No endorsement or commercial track record is claimed.

## Fonts

Barlow Regular, Barlow SemiBold and Barlow Condensed ExtraBold are unchanged font files copied from the frozen Begin dev.17 pack. Their original official Google Fonts source URLs, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes are in [FONT_SOURCES.json](FONT_SOURCES.json).

Complete original notices and license text are bundled in [Barlow-OFL.txt](assets/fonts/Barlow-OFL.txt) and [BarlowCondensed-OFL.txt](assets/fonts/BarlowCondensed-OFL.txt).

## Local estimates

This site prepares an estimate in your browser. It does not submit artwork, collect payments or place an order. A kept estimate uses only the storage entry `overprint.edition.v1` on this device and browser.
