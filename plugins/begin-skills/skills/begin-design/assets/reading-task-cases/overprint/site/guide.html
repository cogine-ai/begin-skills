01

Prepare an edition

Open the estimate desk. The atlas example is ready to use: A small weather atlas, 120 copies, 2 colors, heavy stock. Its note is “Estimate only; confirm the artwork before printing.”

Enter your own project title, a whole quantity from 60 to 2,000, 1 to 3 colors, and either standard or heavy stock. The title must contain text. The note is optional.

Select Prepare estimate. The completed ticket shows the title, all three choices, the per-copy print rate, each fee, the print subtotal, total and your note. For the untouched atlas example, the total is $99.40 USD.

02

Every part of the price

The studio fee is $25.00. Each color adds a $12.00 setup fee. The print rate starts at 18¢ per copy, adds 7¢ per chosen color, and adds another 10¢ for heavy stock.

Print rate, in cents

18 + (7 × colors) + stock extra

Stock extra = 0 for standard, 10 for heavy

Total, in cents

2,500 + (1,200 × colors)
+ (quantity × print rate)

The calculation uses whole cents throughout. A dollar amount is the cents divided by 100, shown with two decimal places.

The atlas example, line by line

Print rate
18 + (7 × 2) + 10 = 42¢
Studio fee
2,500¢
Color setup
1,200 × 2 = 2,400¢
Print subtotal
120 × 42 = 5,040¢
Total
9,940¢ = $99.40 USD

This is an estimate for the stated edition choices. It does not confirm artwork suitability, finishing, delivery or a printing date.

03

Change a choice

Editing any field marks the earlier prepared ticket Out of date. The ticket stays visible so you can compare it with your next idea. Prepare again to replace it with the current choices.

An empty, fractional or out-of-range quantity cannot be prepared. Neither can an empty title, an unsupported color count or an unknown paper stock. The field message explains what to fix. Keep and download remain unavailable until the current fields have a valid prepared estimate.

Changing a field back to its earlier value still needs a fresh preparation. Your kept copy is untouched by edits.

04

Keep it or take the text

Keep this estimate deliberately saves the latest valid prepared edition in this browser on this device. It replaces the earlier kept estimate. The confirmation appears only after the save succeeds.

Download text creates overprint-edition-estimate.txt, a complete UTF-8 text file with the project title, choices, full arithmetic and note. Expand Read the exact download text to inspect those same words before downloading. The file has no image or payment link.

Keeping and downloading are separate actions. An estimate stays available for download even if this browser refuses local storage. In that case, the desk explains that keeping failed; use the text download for a copy you control.

05

Recover, reload or reset

Recover kept estimate restores the kept fields and their exact prepared ticket. It replaces the work currently at the desk, including any unfinished edits.

On reload, the atlas fields appear first. If a valid kept copy is available, a message offers recovery. It is never opened automatically; select Recover kept estimate when you want it.

Reset to atlas example restores the original atlas fields and clears the current prepared ticket. It preserves the kept copy, which you can still recover. Prepare again to create the atlas ticket.

If the browser blocks storage or a kept copy cannot be read, the desk keeps your current work and tells you what happened. An unreadable kept copy is never substituted for your current fields.

06

Forget the kept copy

Forget kept estimate removes only the estimate kept by this OVERPRINT site. It leaves the fields and current prepared ticket in place and leaves other websites' stored information alone.

A successful removal disables recovery. If the browser refuses the removal, the desk says so and continues to offer the known kept copy. You can also remove this site's saved data through your browser settings. Downloaded files stay where you put them.

07

Bring the artwork into the conversation

Use the prepared estimate as a brief, then agree the production details with your printer. Bring these alongside it:

  • The full project title, final print size and intended use.
  • Your artwork and a clear list of the colors you intend to print.
  • The chosen stock, run size and any finishing or packing requirements.
  • The date you need the edition and where it needs to go.

Ask the printer to confirm file preparation, artwork suitability, final price and timing before printing. This fictional studio desk provides a planning estimate; it does not accept artwork or place an order.
