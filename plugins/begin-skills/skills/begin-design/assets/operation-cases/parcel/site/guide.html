How to play

Make yourself
a little space

Fill all twenty squares with the five pieces. Each uses four squares. They can turn, but cannot overlap or reach outside the box.

Continue your puzzle ↗
01

Choose, turn, place

Choose a lettered piece from the tray. Its full name, current angle and location appear beside the controls. The same letter and color identify it on the board.

Use Rotate for a clockwise quarter-turn. Then tap an empty board square. The dotted corner is the top-left of the shape’s rectangular frame; it can be empty on an irregular shape. That is the corner you are positioning.

Choose another piece returns you to the tray. The board, Rotate and current-piece feedback form one compact panel, so you can inspect a turn without leaving the box.

To move a placed piece, select it in the tray and choose a new square. You can also drag a piece from the tray or its occupied board squares. Release inside the board to try the move.

A move outside the box or through another piece is rejected. The previous valid placement stays where it was.

Use the keyboard

Tab / Shift + Tab
Move between the tray, board and controls. The tray has one Tab stop: use its arrow keys to choose A–E, then Tab reaches the box. The next Tab reaches Rotate. Shift + Tab reverses this route.
Arrow keys
In the tray, choose the previous or next piece. On the board, move the dotted anchor one square.
Enter / Space
In the tray, select the focused piece. On the board, place it at the focused square. With no selection, select the piece occupying that square.
R
Rotate the selected piece. From the tray, this also moves focus into the box so you can inspect the turn.
Escape
Cancel a drag or deselect the current piece.
Delete / Backspace
Return the selected piece to the tray while the board is focused.
02

Leave room to change your mind

Undo restores the last accepted placement, turn, return or reset. The latest eighty changes are kept. A rejected move adds no history.

Return to tray lifts the selected piece out of the box while keeping its angle. Reset empties the box and returns all five pieces to their starting angle. You can undo a reset.

The goal is a full board, with every piece placed legally. There is no timer or move limit. Similar shapes are still separate lettered pieces, so your solution may differ from someone else’s.

03

Come back to the same box

Each accepted change is saved automatically in this browser, on this site. Reload the page, visit this guide and return, or close the tab: your current arrangement and undo history recover when browser storage is available.

Check the recovery message beneath the puzzle. Private browsing, cleared site data or a blocked/full store can remove that protection. If saving fails, your puzzle remains usable in the open tab. The guide opens in another tab to keep your work available.

Export arrangement downloads a JSON file containing the board dimensions, every piece’s name, angle, position and occupied squares, plus whether the board is complete. It exports your current arrangement, including an unfinished one. It does not send anything to a service.

This version has no file-import control. Keep the file as a readable record; automatic continuation uses this browser’s saved state.

04

Need a small nudge?

Start at an edge and keep an eye on the empty space. A one-square pocket can be harder to fill than it looks.

Show one opening move

Place A · Apricot at the top-left square, at 0°. Its three-square edge sits along the top of the box. The puzzle has at least one complete arrangement from that opening.
