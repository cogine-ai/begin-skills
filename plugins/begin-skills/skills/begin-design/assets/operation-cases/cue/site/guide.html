Four cues, your own pace.

Open the timer →

1 / Shape your programme

On the timer page, edit the four cue names and their durations. Give each cue a short name you can recognise at a glance. Use whole seconds from 5 to 180, in steps of 5.

The starting programme is Welcome (35 seconds), Story (50), Demonstration (65) and Close (30): three minutes in total. To try the whole flow quickly, set all four durations to 5 seconds.

Changes save as you type when browser storage is available. A field message explains any value that needs correcting before you start or export.

2 / Take your place

  1. Choose Start rehearsal. The large clock counts down the current cue. At zero, the next cue starts automatically.
  2. Choose Pause rehearsal to hold your place. Resume rehearsal continues from that point.
  3. Choose Next cue to skip the unused time in the current cue. While paused, the next cue also stays paused.
  4. When you are finished, choose Reset rehearsal to return to the first cue and edit again. Your cue names and durations stay in place.

Programme left is the time still planned. Run elapsed is the time you have actively rehearsed, excluding pauses and skipped cue time. The two need not add up to your original total.

3 / Come back or keep a copy

Your programme, unfinished edits and rehearsal position are kept in this browser on this device. Follow the guide link, return, or reload to continue. A running rehearsal advances by the time you were away, including across cues. A paused rehearsal stays paused.

Check the local-save message below the timer. If saving is unavailable, keep the timer tab open and export your programme. Links away from the timer open a separate tab in that situation. Return to the original timer tab to continue your rehearsal.

Export programme JSON downloads a real file with the four cue names, their durations in seconds and the total. It is a portable programme record you can read or use elsewhere. It does not contain rehearsal progress, and Cue does not currently import it.

A practice tool, in this browser

Cue is silent. There are no sounds, notifications, accounts or server uploads. Clearing browser storage or changing browser, device or site address can remove access to a saved rehearsal. A private browsing session may clear it when closed.

The timer uses the device’s clock to recover time after an interruption. Device clock changes can affect the result, and background tabs may update their display late. It catches up when active again. Keep the page visible for rehearsal cues; do not rely on it for broadcast timing or precise coordination between devices.

For keyboard use, Tab moves through links, buttons and editable fields. Enter or Space activates a focused button. The screen reader announces cue and control changes, without speaking every second of the countdown.
