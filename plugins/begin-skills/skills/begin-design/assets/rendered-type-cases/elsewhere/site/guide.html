Follow a path, change your mind

You read as Inez Vale. At the end of each full scene, choose one of two actions. Your choice takes you to the next scene; nothing moves until you choose. Each route reaches an ending after four choices.

Back one scene returns to the previous decision. In Your route, select any visited scene to return further. That scene becomes the end of your current path. All scenes and choices after it are removed, so the next choice begins a new continuation.

At an ending, Choose another ending returns to the final decision. To explore different clues earlier in the story, use your route or Start story over. There are three endings; there is no score and no right answer.

Keep the words you carried

  1. In Your reading, to keep, enter a journey title. You can add a personal note; it appears above the scenes in your keepsake.
  2. Select Prepare this reading. The result includes every visited scene in order, its full prose, each chosen action, and your ending. If you stop before an ending, it clearly says Unfinished.
  3. Read the prepared result. Use Keep in this browser to leave a recoverable copy here, or Download reading .txt for a plain-text file you can open elsewhere. Both actions appear again after the last scene.

A change to your title, note, or route makes the prepared result out of date. Its old text stays visible, but Keep and Download pause until you prepare again. A blank title also blocks Prepare. Add a title, then prepare the current reading.

Read the exact download text shows the precise text offered in the file. The download uses UTF-8, which preserves accents and punctuation. It contains your title, note, route, and prose; it does not include unvisited scenes.

A bookmark on this device

The reader sends no personal data or reading choices to a server. It has no account, analytics, remote fonts, audio, or automatic playback. It writes to this browser only when you choose Keep in this browser. Keeping another reading replaces the previous kept one.

Reset reading clears the open reading, including its prepared text. Your kept copy stays here. Recover kept reading replaces the open reading with that copy, restoring its route, title, note, and prepared text.

Forget kept reading removes the browser copy. It leaves your open reading and any downloaded file alone. Reset, Recover, and Forget each explain what will change before you confirm.

A browser may block or clear local storage, especially in private browsing. If keeping fails, the reader tells you. Download a file if you want a copy outside this browser. A kept reading does not move between devices.

Leaving the reader without keeping does not preserve the open reading. Recover is deliberate: reopening the page starts a new reading and offers your kept copy when one is available.

A small original fiction

Platform Seven, Inez Vale, and everyone she meets are fictional. This is a complete short branching story made for ELSEWHERE. The words and station illustration are original. Your choices select written scenes; they do not request a story from an online service.

The story contains bereavement, a flood mentioned in the past, and a choice about memory. It has no graphic violence. You can read slowly, stop at any scene, or prepare an unfinished keepsake.

Keyboard readers can use Tab to reach the choices and Enter or Space to select them. After a choice or revisit, focus moves to the new scene title. The route and exact-text sections are standard disclosures. No sound is needed.
