# Reading priority

Use this when the direction fits the subject but a guide, invitation or prepared artifact loses meaning or rhythm. Inspect the wording in the state the visitor actually reads. A generated title and an empty desk can use different markup.

## Read phrases at the widths where they change

The smallest width is not always the hardest phrase test. Overprint kept “WORK WITH” together at 320px but stranded “WITH” at 390px. Compare the supported narrow view with another width where the phrase changes lines. Group only a useful short phrase or proper name; leave its surrounding text free to wrap.

An optional visual break needs ordinary source whitespace. For example:

```html
<h1>Working <br class="wide-break">guide</h1>
```

The space survives when CSS hides the break. Check the resulting phrase and its visible line gap. A block phrase span beside a preserved whitespace text node can create an extra anonymous line box. In such a title, putting the ordinary space inside the first span preserves the complete wording without that additional line. Keep arbitrary edited titles, including their intentional newlines, complete.

## Give the prepared reading its own review

Inspect its title, state, actions and first useful record together. A short action beside an icon may wrap while its neighbor stays on one line; adjust that local arrangement or let actions stack. Compare the actual generated result, not just the homepage's version of its title.

Bound sustained prose within a broad artifact. Common Tongue aligns its full editorial note with the English reading track instead of stretching it across both languages. Its numbered pairs preserve complete paragraphs and ordinary accents. If a generated identifier touches its label despite a spacing rule, inspect the actual node and its inline styles.

After reading to the end, look for the action the copy promises. A four-pair proof can span several phone viewports. Its second Keep/Download row uses the same prepared state as the opening actions, including stale and invalid disabling. A compact estimate does not automatically need the same repetition.

## Keep a unit attached to its value or column

Wavebench gives the continuous graph a fixed, labeled scale and keeps all sixteen sampled values in a structured table. Its raw-text disclosure is a separate inspection surface. Preserve the exact exported text while giving its column labels an inspectable line; do not strand a unit under another column or shrink the main reading to accommodate the raw format. Short formula groups such as `/ 180` can stay together while the surrounding expression wraps.

Review keyboard focus separately from normal, secondary and disabled controls. A visible focus ring should communicate the actual focused control. A selector that accidentally applies it to every button defeats that distinction.

## Let the visitor's material lead

Table Nine gives guests the particular evening, meal and table scene before the organizer's seating operations. Its service record gives full names, dietary notes and seat relationships priority. Reordering that material changed the encounter while retaining the chosen families. A new font or palette chosen only to make a case set look varied does not establish better fit.

The original three clustered around print and editorial; Wavebench was added after that set review, and three makers independently selected the Barlow display/reading pair. Its digital working surface adds a different task relationship, not evidence of broad font diversity.

The existing pack already discussed spaces, phrase grouping, reading measure and populated results. This study turns their missed application into inspectable examples. A first phrase repair also introduced excess line spacing; the preserved intermediate render informed a second repair. Native downloads, full reading paths, byte inventories and independent pixel criticism have separate receipts. This small internal study establishes particular repairs, not causal improvement or consistently excellent first drafts. Newly added examples require future transfer evidence.
