# Compose contrasting tasks

Use this when familiar warm typography or a repeated image/text split is limiting a new website. Start with the visitor's material and operation. A different palette alone does not create a different composition.

## Give the material a useful relationship

Three complete original cases show distinct choices:

| Material and operation | Case | Relationship worth inspecting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Astronomy programme and local itinerary | [Night Signal](../assets/contrast-cases/night-signal/site/index.html) | Stepped words and an orbital drawing share one night field. The programme continues across that field; the task organizes actual times and clashes. |
| Industrial objects and workspace comparison | [Form Bureau](../assets/contrast-cases/form-bureau/site/index.html) | Unequal specimen sizes, silhouettes and material surfaces make an object mosaic. A compact register and selected comparison preserve full names, measurements and reference prices. |
| Audio documentary production and weekly assignments | [Signal Desk](../assets/contrast-cases/signal-desk/site/index.html) | A broad operating sheet explains the product through actual episodes, editors, days and minutes. Phone views become episode slips, a full editor and day rows. |

Each directory contains `site/index.html`, `site/demo.html`, `site/guide.html` and six actual openings in `captures/`: `home`, `demo` and `guide`, each at `desktop` or `phone`. Inspect the relevant pixels before borrowing source. The original examples use fictional records and local browser state; their models belong to those tasks.

Keep a shared field when its elements jointly explain the subject. Give unlike objects different space when their shape or hierarchy warrants it. Let an operating view carry the opening when the actual work is the strongest explanation. Carry the chosen relationship into the working page and guide; the homepage arrangement need not repeat there.

## Reflow the relationship before shrinking the type

Night Signal's first phone planner retained checkbox, time and prose lanes from desktop. At 390px, only 234px remained for descriptions, with 12px prose and 9px metadata. The revision puts the title, time range and supporting text in one wider column beside the checkbox. Descriptions become 14px and conditions 12px while times remain attached to their session.

Use this when desktop utility lanes consume the reading measure. Keep the identifier or choice control beside the relevant content, then move time, units or conditions below its title. Inspect the complete row and a meaningful conflict at the actual narrow width. A smaller font can hide the spatial problem.

## Preserve scanning anchors and access to selected work

Form Bureau's phone register lost the desktop `mm` heading. Its prices also had a different edge from the other values. The revision supplies a local unit label and a right numeric anchor. A desktop percentage width had to be cleared when that cell became a phone grid; changing its child alignment alone still squeezed the label and price together.

Keep only a short final noun and its link arrow together when an arrow becomes an isolated line. Let the rest of a long name wrap. Broad `nowrap` rules can create a new narrow-screen failure.

The selected comparison begins after a long object catalogue. A compact phone link appears only after selection and shows the current count. A local return leads back to the objects. This is useful for a long selection task; a short page may need only an ordinary section link. Reserve space for a fixed control and inspect it while browsing, comparing and preparing. Its continuing presence costs screen space.

## Put the next operation beside a current result

Signal Desk's first current plan placed Keep and Download below the complete exported text. The revision puts them beside the prepared identity, before the preview, and quiets repeated preparation once the result is current. Phone generation now leaves both operations visible in the inspected view.

Check the actual post-action viewport, focus and enabled state. Moving markup does not establish that the user sees the operation. A stale result should preserve its earlier content and explain the changed basis while blocking operations that require a current version.

## Keep a long guide usable during the task

Use a fitting desktop contents rail where the viewport can contain it. On a long phone guide, a local contents return can restore access to the steps. An action section may also need its own working-page link when the opening's link is far away. Keep these routes conditional: adding controls to every short passage can interrupt reading.

For a sticky rail or animated anchor, compare actual settled pixels with the DOM geometry. This study found native frames that omitted the rail despite its correct measured position. A viewport redraw recovered it at one position; the desktop end-position capture remained inconclusive. Preserve the conflicting frames and accepted replacements separately, and bound the conclusion to visible evidence.

## What this study establishes

Three isolated builders used the exact dev.13 archive with explicit contrasting briefs and resource directions. First sources and native renders were retained before independent criticism. The final cases add focused repairs; they support these guided adaptations and specific observations.

All three builders selected Space Grotesk with IBM Plex Mono. Their different roles, density and compositions do not establish broad font selection. A new subject still needs actual letterform judgment and useful alternatives when the choice is open. These cases were added after their builds and have not yet been tested as new inputs. Internal criticism does not establish beginner acceptance, consistently excellent first drafts, all-model quality or commercial results.
