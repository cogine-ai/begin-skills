# Choose from the brief

Use this resource when the brief leaves fonts and composition open. Begin with the visitor's material and task: objects to compare, quantities to calculate, rules to read, or another useful outcome. Consider a few relevant arrangements and type roles. Choose the relationship that makes the subject recognizable and the task clear; unfamiliar fonts or an unusual layout are not goals by themselves.

The optional original cases below began with business material and functional requirements. Their makers received no font, palette, layout or visual reference recommendation. Each complete first source was preserved before rendering, then independently reviewed and refined. These are three internal examples from one Agent environment. All needed some refinement; they do not establish consistently excellent first drafts or a causal effect from this resource.

## Review the choice and its execution separately

- Judge the actual letters with the subject's full names, rules and numbers. A suitable family can still have poor size, weight, line breaks or hierarchy. Replace the family when the letters conflict with the subject or reading task, not merely because the result needs polish.
- Align comparable facts after accounting for image slots, inner padding and long names. A shared outer grid does not guarantee shared baselines.
- Give prepared work its own useful reading hierarchy. A polished homepage does not finish the document, comparison or result that a visitor actually came to make. Keep exact export text inspectable when the visual output is structured differently.
- Inspect the entire populated result. A nested scrolling area can conceal later content even when the page itself has no overflow. Use continuous reading or an explicit, tested continuation cue.
- Keep small ranges, quantities and units together locally. Reflow their surrounding group rather than applying no-wrap to an entire heading or paragraph.
- Review every heading's punctuation and supporting line deliberately. Preserve sentence punctuation where it carries meaning; do not add a final stop or subtitle by habit.

Apply these decisions to the actual content, task states and phone guide. Copying a case's palette and headline does not transfer its useful relationships.
