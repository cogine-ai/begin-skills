# Typography that belongs to the subject

Use this when the actual letterforms, line breaks or alignment weaken an otherwise useful composition. Preserve the accepted identity and content. A type change can alter the character and proportions of every page; develop the important relationship before extending it.

## Choose letterforms in real wording

Inspect the complete headline, characteristic letters, proportions and stroke contrast at the intended size. Explain what the type contributes to this subject: an intimate listening evening, a precise technical tool or sustained editorial reading can need different emphasis. A familiar family is appropriate when its forms support that purpose. A reference's supplied font remains an editable choice.

Separate display, reading and utility roles. An expressive title does not require expressive instructions, times or controls. Read several paragraphs in the body face and inspect the actual labels and numbers. Tune available weight, width, tracking and line height together; different families at equal CSS sizes can have different visible heights and density. Compare plausible alternatives when the direction remains unresolved, keeping the wording and imagery fixed. A small accepted repair needs only its relevant decision.

## Compose the phrase and its anchors

Choose display breaks by meaning and visual rhythm. Preserve useful phrases and give the lines deliberate emphasis. Let phone widths use a different grouping when necessary; test around the point where it changes. Balanced wrapping can help, but inspect the resulting words. Retain spaces when optional breaks or spans reflow. Keep a title's punctuation and supporting text only when they serve the wording.

Align related copy, rules, images and controls through visible anchors. Inspect letter edges and baselines as well as CSS boxes. Make a small optical correction when it improves the relationship, then check clipping, spacing and the control's usable area. Comparable times and quantities need consistent columns and recognizable labels. Centered text can suit an isolated announcement; a sequence may need a shared reading edge.

## Worked refinement: Side A

This study refined the existing fictional listening evening. It preserved the photograph, wording, colors, body face and itinerary behavior while comparing these display specimens:

| Specimen | Actual choice | Local judgment |
|---|---|---|
| Bricolage Grotesque | Weight 800, width axis 85 | Rounded, compact display forms offered a different emphasis from the original sans |
| Archivo | Weight 850, width axis 72 | Condensed forms made the opening feel more like an announcement |
| Fraunces, retained | Masthead weight 650, `SOFT` 10, `WONK` 1, automatic optical sizing | Its contrasting strokes and shaped details suited the record-sleeve character of this composition |

Fieldnotes Sans remained for reading, utility text and task choices. The retained display face received lighter weights in programme and guide headings. Desktop message, facts and action were aligned with the programme's column anchors. At 320 pixels, the masthead needed a smaller local size. An awkward `A two- / session evening` wrap led to the precise guide heading **A two-session plan**; its instruction still names both sessions. The task legend is **Choose your sessions**.

An early low-level `wght` setting overrode ordinary weight choices across display roles. The revision removed that setting and used `font-weight`, retaining the custom axes. Prefer the corresponding standard property when available: [MDN explains the override behavior](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/font-variation-settings). Automatic optical sizing depends on a supporting font: [MDN's optical-size guidance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/font-optical-sizing).

## Inspect the complete revision

After changing type, inspect every affected page and its consequential states: selections, prepared results, conflicts, stale feedback and kept work. Confirm the loaded family, supported axes, language coverage and notices. Review narrow reflow, reading measure and numeric alignment separately from aesthetic preference.
