# Design with the material the owner has

Use the relevant section when a service has little visual material, photographs carry the subject, or a business workspace contains many records. These are conditional composition decisions. Choose the visitor's task before choosing a style.

## A professional service with few assets

Make the offer concrete: who it helps, what work is included, what the owner delivers, and the useful next step. Let typography, alignment and a readable sequence carry the identity. A small meaningful mark can help; a decorative hero is optional.

Give the visitor something that reveals the professional's judgment. A short original instruction before and after revision, an annotated diagnosis, or an explained lesson can be more informative than an invented client wall. Name the artifact's role and retain its actual content. Do not substitute a testimonial or a statistic for missing evidence.

Different information needs different arrangements. A short service list can use ruled rows with concise detail. A process benefits from phases that name an output or decision. Contact should sit beside the offer or the point where the visitor can choose. Repeated contact actions may help a long page; repeated introductions rarely do.

On phone, read the offer, scope, example and action in their actual order. An enormous typographic opening can still postpone all useful information. Keep the expression while letting the visitor recognize what they can buy and how to proceed. In an enquiry preparation flow, distinguish current input, a prepared summary and an actual sent message. Keep useful scope information close to the action.

Keep a choice group's label outside its option grid. Align the options on desktop and give each a readable full-width row when phone text needs it. After preparation, place the resulting brief close to the preparation action; saving and reset controls should not interrupt that relationship. A guide's complete example should include its actual expected output as well as the inputs.

Reference decisions: [Cloudstudio](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/4f1f5169-9a64-4ae7-bd7e-0bf3cc8486e0) makes a deliverable and contact action prominent; [Apple's service section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3a609c66-6654-4f18-93e7-4ea095f79e9c) separates named services and concise detail; [Fiasco](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/628d4c6b-cea8-49f0-88ca-353161a59678) relates named phases to an explanation. Adapt the relationship with the new service's honest material.

## A website led by photography

Assign each photograph a job: introduce the place, reveal the room or work, show a distinguishing detail, or support a practical choice. Sequence these roles before designing repeated image boxes. Inspect the actual pixels; a source label does not establish quality or a suitable crop.

Keep the useful subject in both wide and narrow views. Choose an aspect ratio and focal position for that specific image; use a separate phone composition when the subject requires it. Preserve understandable captions, the room or project identity, and the adjacent decision information. A wide scenic image and a room detail need not share a ratio.

Photography can supply color and visual character while the frame stays quiet. Judge text against the actual light and dark areas at each crop. Place it on a solid reading surface when an overlay obscures the subject or becomes unreadable. An attractive image does not supply missing location, capacity, price basis or enquiry terms.

Carry the photographic identity into the useful inner page, with proportions that serve the task. A room selector should keep its name, capacity and rate near its image and date controls. Distinguish a calculated estimate, a prepared enquiry and a confirmed reservation. Let the guide show the same fields and result the working page actually produces.

Use owner-supplied or appropriately licensed assets and retain their source/notices. Stock imagery can illustrate a clearly fictional example; it is not evidence of the owner's actual property or portfolio. Replace it with representative material before making real-world claims.

Reference decisions: [KOBU's lodging opening](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/79b3c5a4-83c8-46f4-a63a-fcd4697964cd) pairs a place photograph with an offer and action on a solid surface; its [hotel choices](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3cfe935a-4543-4397-b4f5-82128a07251e) attach names, location and rate context to each image. [Cup of Couple](https://styles.refero.design/style/f4e1b510-6085-4fb8-8597-05d479d3c00c) uses a quiet ruled frame, varied photographic subjects and adjacent serif captions. The inspected [Aspelin Reitan](https://styles.refero.design/style/3a331157-f5a5-4640-ada8-8a3ad262ee6a) section places a photographic scene before a quieter contact band. The image's role and caption matter more than reproducing a particular font or palette.

## A dense business workspace

Start with the record and the decision it supports. Choose which fields people need to scan across rows and which belong in a selected detail. Align comparable fields; numeric values benefit from consistent units and tabular figures. Give the record identity, status and primary operation more weight than incidental metadata. Density comes from useful adjacency and proportion, not uniformly tiny text.

Make the scope of each view explicit. A count may describe all records, matching records or a selected group. A filter changes what is visible; selecting a record changes what is inspected. Editing a detail changes a draft until the supported save action succeeds. Keep those meanings separate in the interface and its summary.

Preserve the selected record's identity through detail, edits and confirmation. When an edit moves it outside the active filter, explain the changed visibility and offer a useful way to inspect it. The record has not disappeared from storage. A zero-result filter needs an understandable recovery action; it is different from a collection with no records.

On desktop, a table and adjacent detail can allow scanning and editing together. On phone, retain the key identity/status fields in a useful list, then give the selected detail readable space. If a comparison genuinely needs horizontal scrolling, keep it within a clearly identified region. Do not shrink a whole desktop table until labels and controls lose their meaning.

Judge the editor with its save and cancel actions visible in a useful context. Reduce incidental identity spacing before shrinking controls; a bounded scrolling editor can help when fields genuinely exceed the available height. On phone, a detail reached after a long queue needs an adjacent route back to that queue. Check the route after opening a different record, not only in the initial state.

Keep control groups restrained: collection navigation, filtering, row selection and editing each have their own job. Disabled or secondary controls should not compete with the consequential action. Review empty, selected, edited and saved states using actual long names and values; the same object must remain recognizable in each state.

Reference decisions: the inspected [Asana table](https://mobbin.com/screens/b19c3efa-47d4-469c-8185-66263e3d8714) groups rows by visible status and identifies an active record. [Airtable's project grid](https://mobbin.com/screens/8ad4f505-28a8-4b6b-86a9-58c7d4c4dbd4) aligns typed columns and aggregate information; its [expanded record](https://mobbin.com/screens/0a357715-a165-4684-b958-866607664786) retains the table context while separating named fields and comments. These visible relationships inform an original workspace; the reference's saving behavior has not been exercised.

## What reference evidence can establish

The external references above were visually inspected on 11 October 2026. They establish visible arrangements and content, not mobile behavior, accessibility, conversions, exact font identity or working integrations. Refero's generated descriptions can disagree with its screenshot: one inspected service description claimed no photography while its image contained a large architectural photograph. Prefer the observed scene and keep interpretations attached to their source.

Test an original adaptation in the actual browser, including its core operation, complete phone/desktop pages and guide. Preserve the initial version before critique or correction. Record the material, reference decision and observed improvement; do not turn one successful palette or layout into a requirement for unrelated websites.

Preserve semantic spaces when a heading uses spans or an optional line break. Read the actual phrase after the phone layout removes that break. A desktop composition can hide joined words that only appear during reflow. The original service, inn and production-desk adaptations in `task-cases.md` retain complete source and matching phone/desktop openings for inspecting these decisions.
