## Choose letterforms for their work

Read the actual title, several paragraphs, a control label and the longest useful value. Compare plausible families when the choice is unresolved. Tune each candidate's weight, width, spacing and line height; equal CSS sizes do not produce equal visual heights. A distinctive display face can belong to the subject while a quieter companion sustains instructions.

Keep meaningful phrases intelligible: a serving count and its unit, “Prepare again,” or the clause completing a short headline. Make local size or grouping corrections, retaining semantic spaces when visual breaks disappear. Avoid making an entire long title unbreakable. Read loaded glyphs around the reflow boundary. An isolated hyphen or one-word ending can weaken emphasis without causing overflow.

A final period and a subtitle each need a reason to exist. Recheck assembled text; knowing the rule does not prevent a generated draft from regressing. Preserve punctuation that serves the sentence or accepted voice.

## Give values useful anchors

Name the edge that helps the task: a reading column, the right edge of amounts, the start of units, or the boundary between explanation and action. Tabular numerals do not align adjacent labels or currencies automatically. Inspect real values, including the longest supported value and its unit.

Conditions such as “per begun 1,000 calls” determine the result. When a phone table cannot carry them at readable size, recompose it into explicitly labeled plan rows or another suitable narrow arrangement. Preserve accessible associations and the desktop comparison. Find the content imposing minimum width; hiding overflow or making critical conditions miniature does not repair reading.

Use aligned expected labels and values when a guide asks readers to compare an exported file. Six quantities in a bold paragraph compete for attention. Keep the day label, status and relevant version attached to their value. A long guide can retain compact desktop contents within its bounds and offer a short phone disclosure.

## Inspect the surrounding composition

An illustration needs responsive height as well as width. A landscape SVG can become a tall empty phone surface when its declared height persists. Inspect the subject, then choose automatic height, a deliberate crop or another composition preserving its meaning.

Check nested columns at the actual phone width and just above their breakpoint. A paragraph beside a small sketch may fit yet become a tiring series of short lines. Move the sketch into the reading sequence when that improves the passage.

Place a prepared result near its action in visual and reading order. A static table between Prepare and its result can obscure a successful operation. Keep that reference findable after the work or in a useful disclosure. Give the populated artifact more attention than an empty poster or repeated advice.
