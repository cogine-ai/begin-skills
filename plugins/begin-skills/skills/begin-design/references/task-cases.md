# Cases for different kinds of work

Use a case when its task, material, and information relationships fit the brief. These original fictional sites each include a public opening, a working local example, and its short guide. Their identities and compositions differ because visitors do different work.

## Choose for the task

| Case | Useful premise | Material required | Tradeoff to judge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lantern: precise event inspection** | A technical visitor must choose a record, read its exact evidence, perform an operation, and recognize the changed result | Stable record IDs, real field names and units, explicit original/changed states, and a meaningful inspectable payload | Dense evidence needs careful type sizing; long contracts need a distinct lookup section after the walkthrough |
| **Common Ground: an expressive programme** | A visitor explores a small set of activities, chooses a session and quantity, checks the cost, and keeps a plan | Distinct activities, useful original illustrations, complete offered dates, prices, and clear current/saved choices | The programme invites exploration before form entry; strong system display fonts can render differently across operating systems |
| **Northline: work from a spatial study** | A visitor inspects drawings, compares independently visible roles, and uses a reference to describe their own project | Original subject-specific drawings with dimensions and readable annotations, explicit role patterns, and an exact project/result relationship | Large phone drawings lengthen the route to the form; a prepared summary must visibly become stale after any relevant edit |
| **Handoff Office: a service with little visual material** | A hardware team needs a guide reviewed or one support task handed to another person, and must prepare a concrete review brief | A bounded offer, a recognizable original instruction before/after, exact audience/problem/date fields, and outputs that change with the chosen scope | The instruction artifact supplies useful evidence without photography; longer service explanations and technical guide details may still slow a repeat visitor |
| **Aldermere House: a photograph-led enquiry** | A prospective guest assesses a room, chooses dates and guests, checks an indicative cost, and prepares an enquiry | Inspected photographs with source/usage limits, capacity and access facts, a stated nightly rate, exact dates, and current/prepared distinctions | Crops must retain useful subjects on phone; illustrative stock photos cannot stand in for proof of an actual property or available room |
| **Pressline: a dense business workspace** | A print studio coordinator compares jobs, filters a queue, opens one record, drafts a revision, and recognizes the saved collection's changed totals | Stable job IDs, client/staff roles, comparable status/date/hour fields, explicit collection/filter/marking scopes, and exact before/after values | Phone needs readable job slips and navigation between queue and editor; marking records and opening one must remain visibly different actions |

Read one relevant case and view its corresponding images. Do not combine their decorative treatments by default. Decide what must stay visible or adjacent for this visitor's task before adapting color or typography.

## Evidence and limits

The dev.6 compositions followed fresh builds from a frozen Begin pack, actual browser inspection, and targeted revisions. The initial rendered reviews identified material phone and guide issues. The same independent critic subsequently judged all three revised cases strong enough to represent that limited edition. On 11 October 2026, owner feedback led to a small title/supporting-copy revision: unnecessary title full stops and repetitive introductions were removed, useful task conditions stayed, and matching opening captures were refreshed. That copy revision received local layout/resource checks; the earlier independent rendered judgment is historical evidence rather than a new judgment about these refreshed images.

The 11 October expansion used a separately frozen dev.8 input containing the previous three cases and conditional material/density guidance. Handoff, Aldermere, and Pressline were absent from that input. Each new first draft was frozen before rendered review; all three required targeted revision. Later independent reviewers inspected opening and full-page final captures for each case and found the material initial findings resolved. Those visual judgments concern the shown widths and states; runtime checks are separate. Handoff's phone guide title subsequently received a spacing correction after a source/native-text check found joined words that its capture review had missed. The retained evidence records that correction separately.

[CHECKS.json](../assets/task-cases/CHECKS.json) separates historical browser behavior and rendered-review evidence from current copy, resource, and syntax checks. The archive regression verifies these resources in both extracted delivery formats. [PROVENANCE.json](../assets/task-cases/PROVENANCE.json) records source paths, bytes, hashes, image dimensions, and evidence snapshots. Raw reviews, builder guidance, and the larger capture corpus are excluded. The original three cases were absent from their dev.6 build input. The expansion builders could use those earlier examples, but did not use the three new outputs as input. This demonstrates bounded use of prior guidance and cases followed by review; it does not establish reliably excellent first drafts. Curation checks do not establish forward use, population success, all-host support, or commercial value. An adaptation needs its own review.
