# Type in a new task

Use this reference when adapting a worked type direction to a different subject, especially when the website includes a useful worksheet and an operation guide. Inspect the matching actual openings and source below. Choose the relationship that serves the new material.

## Choose roles with the actual wording

A family name alone does not establish the direction. Consider the actual headline, a representative reading passage, the longest meaningful item name and the figures a visitor must compare. Give each an appropriate voice and size before repeating it across pages.

The cases share some font files while assigning them different jobs. Little Stage gives performance words an expressive serif voice and dates, counts and instructions a plain sans voice. Kiln Notes separates a shaped display face, sustained serif reading and quiet numeric controls. Home Current uses warm statement lettering with sans instructions and amounts. These are subject-specific choices, not three mandatory palettes or a universal serif recommendation.

Check what the reference taught beyond its font choice: the material that leads, the meaning of a line break, the amount/unit relationship, the task's reading sequence and the guide's expected result. A new subject can call for different letterforms even when the information relationship transfers well.

## Keep meanings intact without freezing sentences

Distinguish semantic spaces from visible word boundaries. Little Stage's first phone title contained a real space after italic “October”, but tight tracking made its boundary with roman “at” look fused. A small local separation repaired the actual glyph relationship. The title, other headings and desktop tracking stayed intact.

Keep a short identifier or quantity together when splitting it weakens an instruction: “Clay no. 07”, “500 g” or an exact short control label. Local nonbreaking spaces or short spans can preserve these groups. Let surrounding prose, long names and status sentences wrap. Check the group at a narrow width and the relevant breakpoint neighbors; a whole sentence made unbreakable can create a new failure.

The guide repair preserves ordinary spaces inside its plain-text specimen. Grouping a rendered instruction does not require changing the downloaded artifact's contents.

## Judge the working text too

Prepared values provide stronger alignment evidence than empty placeholders. Kiln Notes' 125 g formula includes 37.5, 43.75, 18.75 and 25 g. Its amounts share a right edge while the unit has a separate readable position. Home Current's narrow result retains 7440 kWh and USD 7440.00 beside their labels and entered conditions. Little Stage's eight-ticket budget retains counts, line amounts and its exact total.

Use representative fractional values, a long real label or a supported large amount when those are part of the task. Inspect the visible relationships rather than treating equal CSS coordinates as sufficient. A line can continue below the viewport; that is different from clipping inside a field or container.

Editable prose has its own space needs. Kiln Notes' expressive note face worked visually, but a 96-character note needed internal scrolling at 320px in the initial field. A local narrow minimum height exposed the ending while preserving the type and behavior. Check the complete field, including its lower edge, with a representative note. Retain normal scrolling or resizing for longer content; showing every possible maximum-length note at once is not the requirement.

## Carry the decision through the guide

Read the complete guide, including recovery and export. Match exact control names, expected values and destinations to the working page. Home Current's recovery exercise now says “Reload the worksheet page”: “this page” was ambiguous to someone reading the guide.

After a repair, capture the affected passage itself. A clicked contents link or a suggestive filename does not prove that the requested passage is visible. Preserve the first source and first images so a successful initial decision can be distinguished from a later correction.

## What the study supports

Three isolated builds used the frozen dev.12 pack and explicitly inspected relevant references. Independent initial criticism supported retaining their principal type and layout decisions, with focused repairs. An anonymous comparison of one identical ceramics brief from dev.11 and dev.12 preferred different areas of each output. It supports useful guided transfer, not a measured causal improvement, automatic discovery or consistently excellent first drafts across users and models. These three curated cases were added after their builds and need their own future adaptation evidence.
