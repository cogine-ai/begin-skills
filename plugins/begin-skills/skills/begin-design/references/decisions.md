# Worked design decisions

These are original adaptation examples informed by reference observations recorded on 2026-10-08. They demonstrate choices to test. They do not establish conversion results or describe completed starter redesigns.

## Service: an independent tutor with limited assets

**Facts supplied:** online maths lessons for secondary-school students; the parent arranges an introductory conversation; no published testimonials or professional photography.

**Reference decision:** the inspected [YLLW introduction](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ce502cd9-c17f-45be-ad46-a91198465bdd) groups a concrete service explanation and meeting action beside a work scene. Its photograph contributes context and credibility.

**Original response:** open with the audience, the lesson offer, and the parent's next action. Pair this with an original annotated lesson example if the tutor can supply one. Use the tutor's explanation to show how a difficult concept becomes understandable. Keep the example identifiable and avoid invented student results.

**Alternative:** a photograph-led introduction becomes useful when the owner has an appropriate image and rights to publish it. Without that material, typography and a real lesson artifact can carry the composition.

**Choices to explain:** a clear reading area for parents, a legible lesson example, familiar mathematical notation, and an action near the offer. Compare a typographic offer followed by the lesson with adjacent offer/example regions when both need to be understood together. Let spacing and emphasis distinguish their jobs. Select the palette and type from the owner's character and reading needs; the reference's exact colors are optional.

**Check:** can a visitor name the subject, intended students, teaching approach, and contact action? Inspect the lesson at narrow widths and confirm the contact destination. Ask the owner whether the result represents their teaching accurately.

## Portfolio: three projects with different levels of material

**Facts supplied:** one detailed case, one short project, and one self-initiated concept.

**Reference decision:** the inspected [Fiasco work section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/4ed7c118-a866-41fa-824c-32a24323c927) combines varied image proportions with captions that keep projects intelligible.

**Original response:** feature the strongest case, then give each project a consistent brief/role/artifact description. Vary the space according to what each case can explain. Label the concept and use original or owner-supplied artifacts.

**Alternative:** equal-sized tiles fit a genuinely comparable collection. A chronological list fits a story in which order matters. Choose from the content rather than stretching all three projects to appear equal.

**Check:** confirm the owner's intended priority, preserve a meaningful mobile reading order, inspect image crops and fallback text, and keep the route to the contact action visible.

## Product: explain one working task

**Facts supplied:** a small planning tool with a locally working task interaction; no customer logos or usage results.

**Reference decision:** the inspected [Remote introduction](https://mobbin.com/screens/b6423c2e-0877-4e18-8c28-80fc2be490b6) gives explanation, interface demonstration, and supporting evidence distinct positions.

**Original response:** state the task and show one understandable sequence using the actual tool. A short statement above a broad demonstration suits work that needs width. Adjacent explanation and demonstration can help when visitors need to read them together. An opening input can lead when its result explains the value. Compare the relevant arrangements with the same material; choose the phone sequence from the task and the context it needs. Keep prototype limitations in useful context.

**Alternative:** a simple input-led demonstration suits a product whose value appears through one request. A static overview suits a task requiring more context. Show the behavior the product really supports.

**Check:** make the demonstration readable and operable; compare its states with the explanation. Do not let a large product frame overflow its containing column. Verify the stated acquisition action.

## Record the useful decision

For a relevant reference, retain its link, what was directly observed, the decision adopted, why it fits this brief, and what still needs verification. Study the composition and implement original source/assets. Keep unverified font, motion, or conversion interpretations explicit.
