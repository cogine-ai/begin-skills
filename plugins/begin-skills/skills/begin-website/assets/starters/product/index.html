A few useful details

Room for questions.

Is Daylight a real product?+

Daylight is an original fictional product concept made for this website starter. The interactive board demonstrates the idea; it is not an account-backed application.

Does the demo save my tasks?+

No. Tasks stay in this browser page’s memory and disappear when you reload. This starter has no account, cloud storage, analytics, or task-sharing service.

What would a full product need?+

A real product could add persistent storage, accounts, and synchronization. Define those requirements and the privacy choices before adding them.

Can I adapt the website to my own product?+

Yes. Edit the product name, benefit, features, demo, and acquisition destination. Describe the capabilities your product actually provides.
