# Third-party font notices

This starter loads two unmodified local font binaries. Each is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Complete copyright statements and license terms are retained:

- Syne: [fonts/syne-OFL.txt](fonts/syne-OFL.txt)
- DM Sans: [fonts/dm-sans-OFL.txt](fonts/dm-sans-OFL.txt)

[FONT_SOURCES.json](FONT_SOURCES.json) records exact binary hashes and original source URLs. No font or image request goes to a remote service at runtime.

The original concept illustrations remain Begin-authored and byte-identical to the previous service starter. Begin-authored copy, artwork and source follow the pack's release licensing decision; the font licenses do not grant a separate license for those materials.
