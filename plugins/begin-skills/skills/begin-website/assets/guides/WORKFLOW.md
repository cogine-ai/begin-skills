# From an idea to your website

## Start with the purpose

Tell your Agent what the website is for and what you want visitors to do. Bring existing words, work, images, or references when available. “I don’t know yet; suggest something” is a useful answer to a design question.

Your Agent can recommend a direction and an appropriate starting point. The service, portfolio, product, and editorial starters have different content structures. They are editable resources, not the boundaries of what you can make.

The Agent should establish the current task's purpose, scope, important behavior, and completion using what you already supplied. A small site can use a short brief; a complex feature may need a separate document. You do not need to prepare a technical specification before starting.

## See something you can react to

The Agent should create new websites in a new directory and give you an actual preview. Existing websites keep their files, instructions, and working stack. A short explanation of typography, layout, or imagery helps you understand the choices without becoming a designer yourself.

Replace illustrative names, projects, and destinations with your own material. Original concept examples show an approach; they should remain identifiable as concepts. Your contact or purchase destination belongs to you and your website.

## Make a useful change

Say what you want to achieve. For example:

> Make this feel more personal and less formal. Keep the content and the booking link.

> Bring the product demonstration earlier. Make the differences between the features easier to understand.

> Improve the phone layout and menu. Keep the desktop direction.

The Agent should inspect and edit the current source, preserve unrelated work, and show the actual change. You can keep refining the same website; you do not need to recreate it for every request.

## Review the design

You can ask for a candid review of the actual preview. The Agent should look at the whole composition, typography, imagery and reading order on phone and desktop, including pages where visitors read, make contact or follow instructions.

> Review this design against what my visitors need. Tell me which parts weaken it and why. Show a focused improvement while preserving my content and accepted brand.

A useful review identifies a visible problem and its effect. Changes may regroup or reorder content rather than simply adjust spacing. Compare the actual revision with the earlier version and keep any remaining tradeoff clear. A build passing and a reviewer preferring the result establish different things.

## Add functionality when you need it

A simple contact destination can use an existing email or booking link. A form that stores enquiries needs a server and persistence. Accounts, payments, uploads, or other services bring their own setup and access requirements. Begin includes professional guidance across these areas and a small local backend example. Your Agent should distinguish included implementations from integrations still to be built.

Read [extension depth](EXTENSIONS.md) and [service recipes](SERVICE_RECIPES.md) when adding these capabilities. Creating a Begin account or sending usage data is not required for the local making flow.

## Write useful articles and documentation

Describe the reader, their question or task, and the material available. A researched article needs a clear argument and support for its factual claims. A manual needs accurate prerequisites, actions, expected results, and recovery. Your Agent should use current sources, keep your voice, and help you inspect the finished reading experience. A full content calendar or CMS is additional scope.

## Publish and continue

Ask the Agent to prepare the website for the hosting destination you choose. It should inspect the output, use the authorized account/project, and check the resulting URL and visitor action. A local preview is not public publication.

Keep a short `BEGIN.md` with purpose, accepted choices, commands, configuration still needed, and the next step. You can say:

> Continue my website from the current files and project note. Show me what is working and help me finish the next step.

If the preview stops or a change fails, ask for recovery from the existing files. The Agent should diagnose the current state and preserve your work. See [troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
