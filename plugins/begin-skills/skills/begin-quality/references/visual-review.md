# Review the actual design

Use this for a visual critique or a substantial design refinement. Keep ordinary mechanical repairs proportionate. If the person asks only for opinions, give the critique without changing their files.

## Give the review a real task

Read the visitor's purpose, central action, complete required content and accepted direction. Inspect actual rendered openings and the important inner pages at representative phone and desktop sizes. A homepage can look convincing while its essay delays reading or its guide hides the first useful instruction. Inspect the full page as well as the opening when the lower content matters.

When comparing a reference, state the role it serves. Judge how the new content and action are composed; differences from the reference are not defects by themselves. Existing brand choices and the person's accepted decisions remain constraints.

For a new direction, inspect the decisive opening or working detail while it is still small enough to change. Use actual text and the required content density. Review whether the visitor can recognize the subject, its useful operation and the next action before extending the design to more pages. Request this early view only when it resolves a meaningful design uncertainty; an accepted composition need not be reconsidered for a small edit.

## Ask for consequential criticism

Identify the few issues that most weaken the result. Give each a visible location, what is happening, and its cost to the visitor. Separate observed facts from taste. Useful questions include:

- What receives the most attention, and does it communicate this website's subject or offer?
- Do the actual letterforms fit the subject? Do display, sustained reading and utility text have appropriate weight, width and contrast?
- Does the longest real item still work in the smallest repeated frame? Check the widest index and complete time or amount/unit group in the chosen face; a non-overflowing page can still merge facts or fragment a title. If a font weight changes its width, inspect that role again.
- Do headline breaks preserve meaningful phrases and emphasis at the inspected widths? Does a changed font alter the intended hierarchy?
- Do related elements share useful visual anchors and baselines? Are labels close to their content, optical corrections deliberate, and comparable dates, amounts and quantities easy to scan?
- At the actual phone width, do nested prose columns sustain reading, intrinsic image dimensions preserve useful subject scale, and numeric conditions remain readable? Identify content-imposed minimum widths before treating a table as responsive.
- When responsive rules remove a line break, does the phrase retain its spaces? Does a form legend sit outside the grid of choices, and does a prepared result follow its action before storage tools?
- Does heading punctuation serve the wording? Does each subtitle or eyebrow add needed information, or repeat its title and delay the actual content?
- Does space distinguish sections and groups, or repeatedly postpone the important material?
- What can a phone visitor actually see and do? Does the object, offer, reading passage or practical instruction arrive at a useful point?
- Do inner pages continue the identity while serving their specific task? Does a selected essay begin reading, and does a selected guide begin helping?
- Does imagery explain something, and do its crop and caption preserve the useful subject? Are missing facts filled with decoration or invented claims?
- Can the product view explain what this tool does beyond an ordinary list or static page? Are its actual work, version, feedback and result intelligibly related, or does the frame mainly display unrelated navigation?

Be direct about weak hierarchy, awkward wrapping, redundant introductions and unearned decoration. Explain why a recommendation improves this task. Generic praise, a numerical beauty score or a checklist pass cannot establish good design.

## Review state and movement separately

Follow the same item through the primary action. Compare its title, version, content, selected styling, feedback, status text and status color. Then inspect a meaningful correction, item switch or reset. A status can have correct text and stale color; a beautiful preview can show a different version from its adjacent comment. Resolve the actual relationship rather than checking each element in isolation.

Exercise the text relationships with relevant supported extremes: a longer note, a next-day finish or a large call count. Keep units, conditions and previous-version labels readable beside their values. For a numeric guide, compare aligned expected fields with the actual result and exported text. Separate an empty-state judgment from a populated artifact's readability.

For a long catalogue, inspect access to selected work after browsing later items. For a complete prepared artifact, check the actual post-action viewport: current identity, next operation and full preview should have a useful order. Inspect a local guide return or working-page link from the section where it is needed. Fixed controls and repeated links also have costs in screen space and reading flow.

At mixed italic/roman boundaries, inspect the visible word separation even when the text contains a correct space. In operating prose, preserve short identifiers and number/unit pairs while letting surrounding sentences wrap. For a styled note field, inspect the complete control with representative entered text; content below the viewport is different from an ending hidden inside the field. Normal scrolling remains useful for longer notes.

If motion contributes to the task, inspect the transition in addition to its static endpoints. Look for overlapping labels, unreadable intermediate frames, jumping alignment, lost focus, and a result that remains unclear after movement stops. Check reduced motion when claiming that path works. A recorded video can demonstrate appearance; test the real page's controls and state separately.

If a moving-scroll capture disagrees with measured sticky-element positions, obtain a fresh native view after movement stops at the same position. Geometry cannot prove that the element painted. Retain the rejected frame and identify its replacement before drawing a conclusion.

## Use another perspective when it helps

A fresh reviewer can challenge the builder's assumptions. If the host provides an appropriate reviewer or separate Agent, give it the brief, accepted constraints, required content and actual renders. For visual judgment, withhold the builder's preferred answer and previous diagnosis. Source or interaction evidence can then resolve suspected functional defects.

A compact request is:

> Review this site's actual phone and desktop renders against the supplied visitor goal. Inspect the homepage and the important inner pages. Identify the most consequential weaknesses in composition, typography, imagery and reading order. Explain concrete alternatives and their tradeoffs. Distinguish visual observations, aesthetic judgments and behavior you have not tested. Preserve the accepted brand and required content.

Use a focused separate pass when a fresh reviewer is unavailable. State that it is self-review; do not present it as independent judgment. A Skill does not create a reviewer, browser or screenshot capability that the host lacks.

## Turn the criticism into a scoped revision

Preserve the current source and relevant captures before editing. Choose changes supported by the critique and the person's goal. Reordering sections, removing repeated introductory statements or changing a crop can matter more than another spacing adjustment. Keep required content and meaningful destinations intact; moving content is different from discarding it.

Inspect the revision at comparable viewports and states. Verify the changed action and nearby navigation. Check whether the original problem improved, what new tradeoff appeared, and whether another page or size regressed. A critique can be wrong; keep the earlier version if the revision weakens the result.

For a dense editor, include save/cancel and the route back to its collection in the inspected view. For a short operating guide, compare a complete fixture's inputs and visible output with the working page. Initial empty-state captures cannot establish either a prepared result's readability or a saved operation's behavior.

When a guide explains edited, prepared and kept versions, make the comparison easy to scan beside the relevant action. Inspect the real versions and exported values separately. For a catalogue, open a second item and inspect the promised interior material; a persuasive cover does not establish a useful product detail. Keep capture records tied to their actual source phase. A screenshot made after a revision handoff needs its own version label even when it resembles the earlier page.

Complete the agreed revision and report remaining material issues. Additional passes need an unresolved problem or a broader request, rather than a promise to iterate until perfect.

## Record the useful result

Keep a concise note with the affected surface, visible problem, chosen change, actual before/after observation and remaining tradeoff. For example, a guide may expose its first instruction earlier while moving its section overview lower. Report both.

Measurements such as overflow, target bounds or the position of the first useful paragraph support specific claims. They do not replace aesthetic judgment. Reviewer preference is separate from the website owner's acceptance, visitor adoption and commercial results.
