# Help shape what comes next

Show us what you made, or tell us where you got stuck. A link, a screenshot or a few words is enough. You do not need to fill out a feedback form.

[Share an experience or report a problem](https://github.com/cogine-ai/begin-skills/issues/new/choose) in the public Begin Skills repository. For a possible vulnerability, use the [private security route](../SECURITY.md) instead of a public Issue.

For example:

> I made a portfolio with Begin. The first preview was easy. I needed help changing the project images. I kept the result, and I wish the image instructions were clearer.

For an installation problem, it helps to include:

- Your Agent, such as Codex or Claude Code.
- How you installed Begin: native plugin, Skills manager or ZIP fallback.
- The Begin version, if you know it.
- What you expected and the short error you actually saw.

Share the parts you choose. Remove credentials, private addresses, full filesystem paths, visitor information and anything else you do not want to publish. Review a screenshot before attaching it. A complete prompt, source archive or full log is usually unnecessary.

Feedback is optional. Begin does not install an automatic reporting service, and your Agent should not send a message or upload a record on your behalf without your instruction. You can keep working without sharing anything.

We’ll keep improving Begin through the work people make with it.
