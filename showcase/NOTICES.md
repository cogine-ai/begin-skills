# Showcase credits

These screenshots show original Begin demonstration websites. The earlier six examples retain their captures from the source distributed with `0.1.0-beta.1`; LIFT, BENCHTIME and CUTLINE add unchanged native captures from the selected final sources included in `0.1.0-beta.2`. The [capture record](CAPTURES.json) identifies each source edition. Commercial brands, shops, products, prices and festival details are fictional. Offset's graphic studies are self-initiated work. Down to the Moon is an independent educational reader and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.

Begin-authored designs, writing, illustrations and source use the repository's [MIT license](../LICENSE), within the [current license scope](../plugins/begin-skills/LICENSE_STATUS.md). Some early example notices describe their former internal-candidate status; the current pack license record explains the later MIT grant. Fixed historical files remain unchanged.

The nine selected examples use original graphics and local fonts. Third-party font software retains its own copyright and SIL Open Font License notices in the included source. The screenshots are raster captures; no font binaries, external photographs or reference-site screenshots are added to this showcase.
